# GOG and Steam Re-Releases

<small style="color: gray">Last updated: October 26, 2026</small>

## Overview

The legal commercial path to playing Sierra games on modern systems runs primarily through two digital storefronts: **GOG.com** (DRM-free) and **Steam**. GOG carries most of the classic Sierra adventure catalogue, packaged with ScummVM or DOSBox; Steam carries a smaller set of the classic collections plus most post-2014 remakes and successors.[^ref-1][^ref-2]

This page is a snapshot of legal availability as checked against the live GOG and Steam store data on **October 16, 2026** (US store), with every store claim rechecked on **October 26, 2026**. The picture changes as rights holders adjust back-catalogue licensing — see [[Corporate Lineage|Corporate Lineage]] for the IP-rights context.

**Verification protocol:** Never trust a GOG link in a citation without first checking the title is actually available. A search such as `site:gog.com "Game Title"` (Brave or Google) is the recommended verification step; GOG redirects dead product links to its catalogue page rather than showing an error.

## GOG.com Sierra catalogue

On October 16, 2026, GOG's catalogue listed 28 products under its "Sierra" developer filter, plus 6 Dynamix and 8 Impressions Games products.[^ref-1][^ref-16][^ref-17] Most classic adventure series are sold as multi-game packs; many other titles are sold individually.

GOG has been moving its Sierra packages to ScummVM. The King's Quest 1+2+3 and Space Quest 1+2+3 pages say "This game is powered by ScummVM"; Space Quest 1+2+3 replaced DOSBox with ScummVM 2.0 in April 2018 and updated ScummVM to 2026.1.0 on February 20, 2026, the same day the Police Quest Collection "Switched emulation from DOSBox to ScummVM".[^ref-18][^ref-19][^ref-20] Gabriel Knight: Sins of the Fathers also "replaced DOSBox with ScummVM 2.0" in an April 23, 2018 update.[^ref-21]

### What's NOT on GOG

- **[[2015 - King's Quest|King's Quest (2015)]]** — Steam only (see below).
- **EcoQuest, Castle of Dr. Brain, The Black Cauldron, Manhunter: New York, Hoyle Classic Card Games** — not sold; each has a GOG Dreamlist entry where users can vote for a re-release.[^ref-11][^ref-27]
- **[[1987 - Mixed-Up Mother Goose|Mixed-Up Mother Goose]]** — not sold on GOG.
- **[[2010 - Gray Matter|Gray Matter]]** and **[[2022 - SpaceVenture|SpaceVenture]]** — no current GOG product; both are on Steam.[^ref-28][^ref-29]
- **Many smaller Sierra-published and acquired-studio titles** (for example Stunt Flyer, and Coktel's A.G.E. and Bargon Attack) — not available from either storefront.
- **Hoyle entries** and **most 3-D Ultra entries** — no Hoyle title turned up in either store's search. The only 3-D Ultra title sold today is [[2007 - 3D Ultra Minigolf Adventures Deluxe|3D Ultra Minigolf Adventures Deluxe]], listed on Steam as "3D Ultra™ Minigolf Adventures" (Wanako Games / Activision); it is not on GOG.[^ref-47]
- **Coktel pre-Gobliiins titles** — 20,000 Leagues, Asterix, Geisha, Cougar Force, etc. (GOG does sell a Gobliiins pack.)
- **Fan remakes** — AGDI and similar studios distribute their remakes from their own sites.

For these titles, the practical legal path is [[ScummVM]] (or DOSBox) with original game data from physical copies the player owns. Many out-of-print titles otherwise survive only through preservation archives and collectors.

## Steam Sierra catalogue

Steam's Sierra coverage is smaller than GOG's but includes the main classic collections.[^ref-2]

### Classic Sierra collections on Steam (Activision)

- **King's Quest™ Collection** (KQ1–7; King's Quest 8 is not included) and **Space Quest™ Collection** (both released on Steam July 23, 2009)[^ref-30][^ref-31]
- **Police Quest™ Collection**, **Quest for Glory 1-5**, **Gabriel Knight 1, 2 and 3** (sold separately), **Phantasmagoria 1 and 2** (all August 29, 2016)[^ref-32][^ref-33][^ref-34][^ref-35]
- **Police Quest: SWAT** and **SWAT 2**[^ref-36]
- Impressions city-builders: **Caesar 3**, **Pharaoh + Cleopatra**, **Zeus + Poseidon** (Emperor is on GOG but not Steam)[^ref-37]

### Later remakes, reboots and successors on Steam

- **[[2015 - King's Quest|King's Quest (2015)]]** — The Odd Gentlemen's episodic reboot; the base game is free, with paid chapters.[^ref-6]
- **[[2018 - Leisure Suit Larry - Wet Dreams Don't Dry|Wet Dreams Don't Dry]] / [[2020 - Leisure Suit Larry - Wet Dreams Dry Twice|Wet Dreams Dry Twice]]** (Assemble Entertainment) — no longer sold. Assemble announced on October 8, 2026 that both "will only be available on Steam until October 23. After that, both games will be removed from the store."[^ref-38] Both were removed from Steam and GOG on October 23, 2026; their Steam pages now say they are "no longer available on the Steam store".[^ref-44][^ref-45][^ref-46]
- **Gabriel Knight: Sins of the Fathers 20th Anniversary Edition** (Phoenix Online Studios and Pinkerton Road, October 15, 2014)[^ref-39]
- **[[2014 - Quest for Infamy|Quest for Infamy]]** (Infamous Quests)[^ref-26]
- **[[2018 - Hero-U - Rogue to Redemption|Hero-U: Rogue to Redemption]]** (Transolar Games)[^ref-25]
- **[[2023 - Gobliiins 5 - The Morgloton Invasion|GOBLiiiNS5]]** (2023) and **[[2026 - Gobliins 6|GOBLiiNS6]]** (2026) — Pierre Gilhodes / Schnibble Productions. [[2009 - Gobliiins 4|Gobliiins 4]] is on neither store.[^ref-40]
- **[[2019 - Mage's Initiation - Reign of the Elements|Mage's Initiation: Reign of the Elements]]** (Himalaya Studios)[^ref-26]
- **[[2010 - Gray Matter|Gray Matter]]** (Jane Jensen's adventure; WizarBox Production / Viva Media on Steam) and **[[2022 - SpaceVenture|SpaceVenture]]** (Guys from Andromeda, December 23, 2025)[^ref-28][^ref-29]
- **[[2015 - Homeworld Remastered Collection|Homeworld Remastered Collection]]** (Gearbox)[^ref-23] and **[[2024 - Homeworld 3|Homeworld 3]]** (Blackbird Interactive; published by Gearbox Publishing)[^ref-48]
- **[[2023 - Pharaoh - A New Era|Pharaoh: A New Era]]** (Triskell Interactive / Dotemu, February 15, 2023)[^ref-10]
- **[[1998 - Half-Life|Half-Life]]** — Valve owns and sells it.[^ref-41]

**No longer on Steam:** [[2013 - Leisure Suit Larry - Reloaded|Leisure Suit Larry: Reloaded]] — its store page says it "is no longer available on the Steam store"; it is still sold on GOG.[^ref-42] The classic Leisure Suit Larry games are gone too: in May 2025 Assemble Entertainment announced that LSL 1, 2, 3, 5, 6, 7 (Love for Sail) and Magna Cum Laude were being removed from Steam, while the GOG versions stayed on sale.[^ref-49] The Steam pages for LSL 1, 2, 5 and 7 now say they are "no longer available on the Steam store".[^ref-50]

### Steam-only vs. GOG

SpaceVenture, Gray Matter, King's Quest (2015) and the GOBLiiiNS5/GOBLiiNS6 sequels are on Steam but not GOG. Hero-U (GOG July 9, 2018; Steam July 10, 2018) and Pharaoh: A New Era (both February 15, 2023) launched on both stores within a day of each other.[^ref-25][^ref-10]

## Modern remakes and re-releases by license category

### Officially published remakes and reboots (post-1999)

- **[[1993 - Gabriel Knight - Sins of the Fathers|Gabriel Knight: Sins of the Fathers — 20th Anniversary Edition]]** (2014) — Remake by Phoenix Online Studios and Pinkerton Road, the studio of Jane Jensen and Robert Holmes.[^ref-7][^ref-39]
- **[[2015 - King's Quest|King's Quest (2015)]]** (The Odd Gentlemen, Activision/Sierra) — Activision announced in August 2014 that "The Odd Gentleman studio will bring back King's Quest"; it was released in chapters from July 2015.[^ref-8][^ref-6]
- **[[2013 - Leisure Suit Larry - Reloaded|Leisure Suit Larry: Reloaded]]** (Replay Games, 2013) — Crowdfunded HD remake of LSL1; GOG describes it as "From original designer Al Lowe".[^ref-9][^ref-43]
- **[[2023 - Pharaoh - A New Era|Pharaoh: A New Era]]** (Triskell Interactive / Dotemu, 2023) — "a remake of Pharaoh (and the expansion Cleopatra: Queen of the Nile)".[^ref-10]

### Fan remakes

- **[[2008 - Quest for Glory II VGA Remake|Quest for Glory II VGA]]** (AGDI) — Free via [agdinteractive.com](https://www.agdinteractive.com), not on storefronts.[^ref-14]
- **AGDI's King's Quest 1, 2 and 3 remakes** — Free via AGDI, not on storefronts.[^ref-14]

Fan-game policy: AGDI, Infamous Adventures, and similar fan-developer studios have released their Sierra remakes free of charge. Commercial titles from the same community (Quest for Infamy, Mage's Initiation, etc.) are original IP that occupies the same design space, not Sierra IP.

## Preservation gaps and recommended actions

For Sierra titles NOT available on GOG or Steam:

1. **Check legal status first** — An out-of-print game is still under copyright; "abandonware" is a community label, not a legal status. Check GOG and Steam before assuming a game is unavailable.
2. **Use [[ScummVM]]** if you own original game data (CDs, floppies) — it runs AGI and SCI-engine titles on modern hardware.
3. **Watch for licensing announcements** — The catalogue still changes: the classic Larry games left Steam after a May 2025 announcement, Leisure Suit Larry: Reloaded is gone from Steam, and Assemble removed its two Wet Dreams games from Steam and GOG on October 23, 2026.[^ref-49][^ref-42][^ref-38][^ref-45][^ref-46]
4. **Vote on the GOG Dreamlist** — The [GOG Dreamlist](https://www.gog.com/dreamlist) lets users vote for titles to receive re-releases. Sierra entries include EcoQuest, Castle of Dr. Brain, The Black Cauldron, Manhunter: New York and Hoyle Classic Card Games.[^ref-11][^ref-27]

## Citation protocol for vault pages

When adding a GOG or Steam URL to a vault page's References section, follow these rules:

1. **Verify the URL resolves** to the product itself, not GOG's catalogue page (a `site:gog.com "Game Title"` search is the fastest way to find the canonical URL).
2. **Use the canonical short URL** — `gog.com/en/game/<slug>` for English; `gog.com/game/<slug>` is the slug-only form.
3. **Include in `## Downloads` section** under a `**Purchase / Digital Stores**` heading, not in the main References list (unless the GOG listing is the source for a factual claim, in which case both).
4. **Update on commercial-availability changes** — A title removed from GOG should be moved to the preservation section.
5. **Never link download copies of games that are still sold.** For out-of-print titles, describe preservation status in plain text.
