# Metaltech Earthsiege Speech Pack

<small style="color: gray">Last updated: October 13, 2026</small>

## Overview

The **Metaltech Earthsiege Speech Pack** is an add-on for [[1994 - Metaltech - Earthsiege|Metaltech: Earthsiege]], developed by Dynamix and published by Sierra On-Line for DOS in 1994.[^ref-1] It shipped on 3.5" floppy disks and adds digitized speech, "like the voices of your commander or your fellow pilots," to the game.[^ref-1]

The pack exists because of how Earthsiege itself was sold. It "works only with the 3.5 floppy version of the game"; the CD version of Earthsiege already has this feature.[^ref-1] Sierra sold the pack separately, and it was included in later CD releases.[^ref-10] Wikipedia gives the same account: a speech pack was released in 1994, "it adds digitized speech for some characters," and "the CD-ROM version was released with this feature."[^ref-9]

## Story Summary

The Speech Pack adds no story content of its own. It voices the existing characters of [[1994 - Metaltech - Earthsiege|Metaltech: Earthsiege]], where players pilot giant HERCs against the Cybrids, machines that rebelled against humanity.[^ref-5] Tribes Depot describes the pack as providing "audio speech for the characters in the game to accompany the standard text speech."[^ref-10]

## Gameplay

### Interface and Controls

The pack changes nothing about how Earthsiege plays. It installs alongside the floppy version of the base game and adds speech audio.[^ref-1][^ref-10] PCGamingWiki lists the base game's storage requirement as 27 MB, with an "extra 8 MB needed for Speech Pack."[^ref-17] In the game's preferences, Speech can be set to "Text and Voice."[^ref-17]

## Reception

### Contemporary Reviews

No contemporary review of the Speech Pack on its own has been found. MobyGames has no critic score for it.[^ref-1] The base game was reviewed. *Computer Gaming World* gave it 2.5/5 and called it "the best attempt yet at creating a futuristic simulation of giant armored combat." *Next Generation* gave it 4/5 and called it "Without a doubt, the finest mech simulator available."[^ref-9]

## Development

### Origins

Little is documented about how the pack was made. MobyGames lists no credits for it.[^ref-1] For the CD-ROM base game, which already had speech, MobyGames credits Jan Paul Moorhead as audio director and Ken Rogers for "Sound FX and Digitized Speech."[^ref-5]

### Version History

| Version | Date | Platform | Notes |
|---------|------|----------|-------|
| Speech Pack | 1994 | DOS (3.5" floppy) | Separate add-on for the floppy version of Earthsiege[^ref-1][^ref-10] |
| Earthsiege CD-ROM | 1994 | DOS (CD-ROM) | Includes the Speech Pack and a video intro sequence[^ref-17] |

Sources disagree on the release year. MobyGames and Wikipedia say 1994,[^ref-1][^ref-9] but the blog Remember PC DOS Games says the speech pack was released in 1995.[^ref-4]

## Legacy

### Collections

The CD version of Earthsiege included the Speech Pack, along with a video intro sequence that replaced the floppy version's slides.[^ref-17] The CD version of the 1995 [[1995 - Metaltech - Earthsiege Expansion Pack|Expansion Pack]] added new voiceovers for its own mission briefings. Unlike the base game's speech, those voiceovers were not available separately.[^ref-17]

Hi-Rez Studios re-released Earthsiege for free in 2015. PCGamingWiki notes that the Speech Pack is "included in the free version."[^ref-17]
