# Metaltech Earthsiege Speech Pack

<small style="color: gray">Last updated: October 23, 2026</small>

## Overview

The **Metaltech Earthsiege Speech Pack** is an add-on for [[1994 - Metaltech - Earthsiege|Metaltech: Earthsiege]], developed by Dynamix and published by Sierra On-Line for DOS in 1994.[^ref-1] It shipped on 3.5" floppy disks and adds digitized speech, "like the voices of your commander or your fellow pilots," to the game.[^ref-1]

The pack exists because of how Earthsiege itself was sold. It "works only with the 3.5 floppy version of the game"; the CD version of Earthsiege already has this feature.[^ref-1] Sierra sold the pack separately, and it was included in later CD releases.[^ref-10] The Earthsiege manual describes it as an optional pack that "adds digitized voices to game messages and dialog," which DOS users "can purchase... separately from Sierra or at their software dealer," and notes that "the CD version contains the complete Speech Pack supplement."[^ref-44] Wikipedia gives the same account: a speech pack was released in 1994, "it adds digitized speech for some characters," and "the CD-ROM version was released with this feature."[^ref-9]

## Story Summary

The Speech Pack adds no story content of its own. It voices the existing characters of [[1994 - Metaltech - Earthsiege|Metaltech: Earthsiege]], where players pilot giant HERCs against the Cybrids, machines that rebelled against humanity.[^ref-5] Tribes Depot describes the pack as providing "audio speech for the characters in the game to accompany the standard text speech."[^ref-10]

## Gameplay

### Interface and Controls

The pack changes nothing about how Earthsiege plays. It installs alongside the floppy version of the base game and adds speech audio.[^ref-1][^ref-10] PCGamingWiki lists the base game's storage requirement as 27 MB, with an "extra 8 MB needed for Speech Pack."[^ref-17] The manual gives about 23 MB installed and 27 MB free during installation, adding: "You will need another 8 MB to load the optional Speech Pack."[^ref-44] In the game's preferences, Speech can be set to on-screen "Text, Text and Voice, or Voice only"; the manual notes that "Voice options require the CD version or optional DOS version Speech Pack."[^ref-44][^ref-17]

## Reception

### Contemporary Reviews

No contemporary review of the Speech Pack on its own has been found. MobyGames has no critic score for it.[^ref-1] The base game was reviewed. *Computer Gaming World* gave it 2.5/5 and called it "the best attempt yet at creating a futuristic simulation of giant armored combat."[^ref-9][^ref-47] *Next Generation* gave it 4/5 and called it "Without a doubt, the finest mech simulator available."[^ref-9][^ref-48]

## Development

### Origins

Little is documented about how the pack was made. MobyGames lists no credits for it.[^ref-1] For the CD-ROM base game, which already had speech, MobyGames credits Jan Paul Moorhead as audio director and Ken Rogers for "Sound FX and Digitized Speech."[^ref-5] The Earthsiege manual gives Rogers the same credit.[^ref-44]

### Version History

| Version | Date | Platform | Notes |
|---------|------|----------|-------|
| Speech Pack | 1994 | DOS (3.5" floppy) | Separate add-on for the floppy version of Earthsiege[^ref-1][^ref-10] |
| Earthsiege CD-ROM | 1994 | DOS (CD-ROM) | Includes the Speech Pack and a video intro sequence[^ref-17] |

The pack dates from 1994. Sierra listed "MetalTech: EarthSiege Speech Pack" alongside Battledrome and EarthSiege in its Holiday 1994 *InterAction* catalogue,[^ref-45] and Ken Williams' release list places it under 1994 as "MetalTech - EarthSiege: Speech Pack (Nov)".[^ref-46] MobyGames and Wikipedia also give 1994.[^ref-1][^ref-9] A 2018 blog, Remember PC DOS Games, gives 1995, which the period sources do not support.[^ref-4]

The pack was still on sale in Europe the following year. Sierra's UK mail-order catalogue list dated 11/08/95 lists "EARTHSIEGE SPEECH PACK (for disk" as an add-on in FE/FF/FG (full English, French and German) at £9.99 for PC 3.5" disks.[^ref-49]

## Legacy

### Collections

The CD version of Earthsiege included the Speech Pack, along with a video intro sequence that replaced the floppy version's slides.[^ref-17] The CD version of the 1995 [[1995 - Metaltech - Earthsiege Expansion Pack|Expansion Pack]] added new voiceovers for its own mission briefings. Unlike the base game's speech, those voiceovers were not available separately.[^ref-17]

Hi-Rez Studios re-released Earthsiege for free in 2015. PCGamingWiki notes that the Speech Pack is "included in the free version."[^ref-17]
