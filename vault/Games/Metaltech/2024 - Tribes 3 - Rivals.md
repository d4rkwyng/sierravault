# Tribes 3: Rivals

<small style="color: gray">Last updated: October 13, 2026</small>

## Overview

Tribes 3: Rivals is a first-person shooter developed and published by Prophecy Games for Windows.[^ref-1][^ref-5] Released into Early Access on Steam on March 12, 2024, it is the latest entry in the Tribes franchise, which began with Dynamix's [[1998 - Starsiege - Tribes|Starsiege: Tribes]], published by Sierra.[^ref-5][^ref-29]

Built on Unreal Engine 5, Tribes 3: Rivals is a team-based capture-the-flag shooter built around the series' jetpack flight and "skiing" movement.[^ref-1][^ref-2][^ref-5]

## Development

### Origins

Hi-Rez Studios bought the Tribes IP from InstantAction in October 2010 and released Tribes: Ascend in 2012.[^ref-29] Prophecy Games was founded in late 2019 as a studio within Hi-Rez and spun off as a fully independent studio in early 2020.[^ref-16] Tribes 3: Rivals was announced in November 2023 and went through several betas before its Early Access launch.[^ref-2][^ref-29] Wikipedia credits Erez Goren as designer and Mick Larkins as programmer.[^ref-1]

Prophecy describes the game as "crafted with input from the TRIBES community", aiming to honor the series' legacy while adding new elements.[^ref-5] At launch, the studio published an Early Access roadmap of new weapons, maps and class abilities, and hoped to stay in Early Access for six to twelve months.[^ref-2]

### Technical Achievements

The game runs on Unreal Engine 5.[^ref-1][^ref-9] Skiing, the series' defining movement mechanic, returns: holding Shift builds momentum while moving downhill, and steeper slopes give more speed.[^ref-26] The game is PC-only on Steam, with dedicated servers in North America, Europe, Asia, Latin America and Oceania; Prophecy has said it is considering PlayStation and Xbox versions.[^ref-27] It uses Denuvo Anti-Cheat, which Prophecy notes is separate from Denuvo's anti-tamper DRM.[^ref-26]

## Gameplay

### Interface and Controls

Holding Shift, the key usually used to sprint in other shooters, makes the player ski down slopes to build speed.[^ref-26] For accessibility, players can change weapon reticle colors and adjust in-game volume.[^ref-26] The game has no passive health regeneration: players regain health by collecting health nuggets dropped by players they eliminate, or by recalling to base.[^ref-26]

### Structure and Progression

Tribes 3: Rivals keeps the core elements of the Dynamix-era games:[^ref-5]

- **Skiing:** Players build speed on downhill slopes by holding Shift.[^ref-26]
- **Jetpacks:** Jetpack flight gives aerial mobility across the battlefield.[^ref-5]
- **Spinfusors:** The series' signature disc launchers return in light, medium and heavy versions.[^ref-26]
- **Capture the Flag:** The core mode, in which teams steal the enemy flag while defending their own.[^ref-5][^ref-27]

Weapons do not need to be unlocked, and all non-cosmetic items are accessible to every player.[^ref-26]

### Puzzles and Mechanics

Players choose from three armor classes:[^ref-26]

- **Light:** The fastest and most agile class, specialized in capturing the flag or chasing enemy flag carriers
- **Medium:** The most balanced and versatile class
- **Heavy:** The most durable class, with some of the hardest-hitting weapons

There are six perks: Pathfinder, Sentinel, Raider, Technician, Doombringer and Juggernaut.[^ref-26] Weapon and equipment categories include spinfusors, launchers, grenades, packs, shields, a sniper rifle, a pistol and a shotgun, the chain gun and the shocklance. All weapons fire projectiles except the shotgun and shocklance, which are hitscan.[^ref-26]

### Maps and Modes

Seven maps were available: Katabatic, Wavemist, DryDock, Dangerous Crossing, Torment, Hollow and Raindance.[^ref-26] Modes include:[^ref-27]

- 16v16 casual Capture the Flag (CTF)
- Ranked 7v7 CTF
- Time trials
- Shooting range
- Tutorial
- Custom lobbies with adjustable team sizes, physics and settings
- Team Deathmatch in custom lobbies on the smaller Honor Dome, Ice Dome and Magma Dome maps

Vehicles were not included during Early Access.[^ref-26]

## Reception

### Contemporary Reviews

Rock Paper Shotgun covered the Early Access launch as "another shot at making Tribes popular".[^ref-2] IGN reviewed the Early Access release on March 15, 2024, giving it a 7 out of 10. Reviewer Travis Northup called it "a rocket-powered sequel that packs some serious horsepower, but its current Early Access options run out of fuel quite quickly."[^ref-28]

The game peaked at 890 concurrent players on launch day, March 12, 2024.[^ref-3] By late June 2024, PC Gamer counted 27 players in the game.[^ref-4]

### Modern Assessment

On June 27, 2024, Prophecy Games posted an announcement on Steam. It said that neither Tribes 3: Rivals nor its other title, Starsiege: Deadzone, "had enough success yet to support the studio and can't be our main development focus right now".[^ref-4] Prophecy said it would support Tribes 3 with "limited updates".[^ref-4] PC Gamer covered the announcement under the headline "The latest attempt to revive Tribes looks cooked after three months".[^ref-4]

## Legacy

### Tribes Franchise Revival Attempts

Tribes 3: Rivals is the most recent release in the Tribes series, following Tribes: Ascend (2012).[^ref-29] Earlier entries were developed by Dynamix, Inevitable Entertainment, Irrational Games and Hi-Rez Studios.[^ref-29] This one comes from Prophecy Games, a studio spun off from Hi-Rez.[^ref-2][^ref-16]

The game kept the classic Tribes formula of skiing, jetpacks and Capture the Flag, but within three months it had too few players to remain the studio's main focus.[^ref-4][^ref-5]

### Community Preservation

The game is still sold in Early Access on Steam.[^ref-5] In October 2026, SteamDB showed concurrent players in the teens and a 24-hour peak in the low forties.[^ref-3] The official website still hosts the game's how-to-play guide and FAQ.[^ref-6]
