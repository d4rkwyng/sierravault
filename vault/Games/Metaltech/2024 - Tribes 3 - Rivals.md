# Tribes 3: Rivals

<small style="color: gray">Last updated: October 23, 2026</small>

## Overview

Tribes 3: Rivals is a first-person shooter developed and published by Prophecy Games for Windows.[^ref-1][^ref-5][^ref-36] Released into Early Access on Steam on March 12, 2024, it is the latest entry in the Tribes franchise, which began with Dynamix's [[1998 - Starsiege - Tribes|Starsiege: Tribes]], published by Sierra.[^ref-5][^ref-29]

Built on Unreal Engine 5, Tribes 3: Rivals is a team-based capture-the-flag shooter built around the series' jetpack flight and "skiing" movement.[^ref-1][^ref-2][^ref-5]

## Development

### Origins

Hi-Rez Studios bought the Tribes IP from InstantAction in October 2010 and released Tribes: Ascend in 2012.[^ref-29] Prophecy Games was founded in late 2019 as a studio within Hi-Rez and spun off as a fully independent studio in early 2020, taking Tribes with it.[^ref-16][^ref-30] IGN reported the full reveal of Tribes 3: Rivals on November 29, 2023. At that point it was a 5v5 game, with vehicles planned for later, and Prophecy expected to launch on PC through Steam and the Epic Games Store, with PlayStation and Xbox versions "at a later time".[^ref-30] The game went through several betas before its Early Access launch.[^ref-2]

Rock Paper Shotgun's notes from the December 2023 alpha playtest called it "heavily Ascend-inspired", noting that it copied over much of Tribes: Ascend's sound effects and music, and that vehicles and the VGS voice-command system were planned but not yet present. The default Capture the Flag mode was 5v5, with a 12v12 option tucked into custom lobbies.[^ref-31] IGN's hands-on with the same alpha build enjoyed the speed of play but worried about "somewhat claustrophobic maps and tiny teams".[^ref-32] In February 2024 the game took part in Steam Next Fest with a free playable demo.[^ref-37] A Steam FAQ posted on March 2, 2024 confirmed the Early Access date of March 12, 2024 and a launch price of $17.99, a 10% discount on the full price of $19.99.[^ref-33]

Prophecy describes the game as "crafted with input from the TRIBES community", aiming to honor the series' legacy while adding new elements.[^ref-5] At launch, the studio published an Early Access roadmap of new weapons, maps and class abilities, and hoped to stay in Early Access for six to twelve months.[^ref-2]

### Technical Achievements

The game runs on Unreal Engine 5.[^ref-1][^ref-9] Skiing, the series' defining movement mechanic, returns: holding Shift builds momentum while moving downhill, and steeper slopes give more speed.[^ref-26] The game is PC-only on Steam, with dedicated servers in North America, Europe, Asia, Latin America and Oceania; Prophecy has said it is considering PlayStation and Xbox versions.[^ref-27][^ref-33] It uses Denuvo Anti-Cheat, which Prophecy notes is separate from Denuvo's anti-tamper DRM.[^ref-26]

## Gameplay

### Interface and Controls

Holding Shift, the key usually used to sprint in other shooters, makes the player ski down slopes to build speed.[^ref-26] For accessibility, players can change weapon reticle colors and adjust in-game volume.[^ref-26] The game has no passive health regeneration: players regain health by collecting health nuggets dropped by players they eliminate, or by recalling to base.[^ref-26]

### Structure and Progression

Tribes 3: Rivals keeps the core elements of the Dynamix-era games:[^ref-5]

- **Skiing:** Players build speed on downhill slopes by holding Shift.[^ref-26]
- **Jetpacks:** Jetpack flight gives aerial mobility across the battlefield.[^ref-5]
- **Spinfusors:** The series' signature disc launchers return in light, medium and heavy versions.[^ref-26]
- **Capture the Flag:** The core mode, in which teams steal the enemy flag while defending their own.[^ref-5][^ref-27]

Weapons do not need to be unlocked, and all non-cosmetic items are accessible to every player.[^ref-26]

### Puzzles and Mechanics

Players choose from three armor classes:[^ref-26]

- **Light:** The fastest and most agile class, specialized in capturing the flag or chasing enemy flag carriers
- **Medium:** The most balanced and versatile class
- **Heavy:** The most durable class, with some of the hardest-hitting weapons

There are six perks: Pathfinder, Sentinel, Raider, Technician, Doombringer and Juggernaut.[^ref-26] Weapon and equipment categories include spinfusors, launchers, grenades, packs, shields, a sniper rifle, a pistol and a shotgun, the chain gun and the shocklance. All weapons fire projectiles except the shotgun and shocklance, which are hitscan.[^ref-26]

### Maps and Modes

The official guide lists seven maps: Katabatic, Wavemist, DryDock, Dangerous Crossing, Torment, Hollow and Raindance.[^ref-26] A further map, Moonrise, was added in the June 17, 2024 patch, which also added NVIDIA Reflex and a friendly-fire mutator.[^ref-35] Modes include:[^ref-27][^ref-33]

- 16v16 casual Capture the Flag (CTF)
- Ranked 7v7 CTF
- Time trials
- Shooting range
- Tutorial
- Custom lobbies with adjustable team sizes, physics and settings
- Team Deathmatch in custom lobbies on the smaller Honor Dome, Ice Dome and Magma Dome maps

Vehicles were not included during Early Access.[^ref-26]

## Reception

### Contemporary Reviews

Rock Paper Shotgun covered the Early Access launch as "another shot at making Tribes popular".[^ref-2] IGN reviewed the Early Access release on March 15, 2024, giving it a 7 out of 10. Reviewer Travis Northup called it "a rocket-powered sequel that packs some serious horsepower, but its current Early Access options run out of fuel quite quickly."[^ref-28]

The game peaked at 890 concurrent players on launch day, March 12, 2024.[^ref-3] By late June 2024, PC Gamer counted 27 players in the game.[^ref-4]

### Modern Assessment

On June 26, 2024, Prophecy Games posted an announcement on Steam. It said that neither Tribes 3: Rivals nor its other title, Starsiege: Deadzone, "had enough success yet to support the studio and can't be our main development focus right now".[^ref-34] Prophecy said it would support Tribes 3 with "limited updates", and gave players a dollar-for-dollar credit, for anything spent on either game before June 27, 2024, towards its next game, Ultra Strikers.[^ref-34] PC Gamer covered the announcement the next day under the headline "The latest attempt to revive Tribes looks cooked after three months".[^ref-4]

## Legacy

### Tribes Franchise Revival Attempts

Tribes 3: Rivals is the most recent release in the Tribes series, following Tribes: Ascend (2012).[^ref-29] Earlier entries were developed by Dynamix, Inevitable Entertainment, Irrational Games and Hi-Rez Studios.[^ref-29] This one comes from Prophecy Games, a studio spun off from Hi-Rez.[^ref-2][^ref-16]

The game kept the classic Tribes formula of skiing, jetpacks and Capture the Flag, but within three months Prophecy said it had not done well enough to remain the studio's main focus.[^ref-34] The June 17, 2024 "Moonrise" update is the last patch announcement in the game's Steam news feed.[^ref-35]

### Community Preservation

The game is still sold in Early Access on Steam.[^ref-5] The official website still hosts the game's how-to-play guide and FAQ.[^ref-6]
