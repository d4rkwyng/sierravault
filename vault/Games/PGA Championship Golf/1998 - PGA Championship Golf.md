# PGA Championship Golf

<small style="color: gray">Last updated: October 13, 2026</small>

## Overview

PGA Championship Golf, also known as PGA Championship Golf '99, is a golf game for Windows developed by [[Headgate Studios]] and published by [[Sierra On-Line]]. MobyGames catalogues it as a 1998 release in the United Kingdom, distributed there by Cendant Software UK.[^ref-35][^ref-36]

This page appears to describe the same product as [[1999 - PGA Championship Golf 1999 Edition]], not a separate game. Sierra Sports announced *PGA Championship Golf, 1999 Edition* in July 1998, with seven courses including Sahalee Country Club, for an October 1998 release.[^ref-37][^ref-38] The game did not ship in North America until late May 1999.[^ref-37] Eurogamer's review of the UK budget re-release says "the game was first released in 1998".[^ref-15] No source found so far describes a 1998 game that is separate from the 1999 Edition. The 1998 date in MobyGames is also not confirmed elsewhere: Wikipedia states only that the game had been released in the UK by September 1999.[^ref-37]

## Gameplay

MobyGames describes the game as having seven championship courses, including Sahalee Country Club and Royal Birkdale, plus twelve game modes such as Skins, Medal and Stableford. It also lists a course editor, more than 15 camera angles and several animated player characters.[^ref-35] These details match the 1999 Edition, which had seven real courses, a fictional eighth course (Jocassee Shoals) built with the Course Architect, and 12 game modes.[^ref-37]

MobyGames lists the technical requirements as Windows 95, an Intel Pentium, 32 MB of RAM and a 4x CD-ROM drive. It also lists mouse support, 640×480 video and Internet multiplayer. The UK release carried an ELSPA 3+ rating.[^ref-35]

## Reception

MobyGames records a critics' average of 84% from three ratings for this entry.[^ref-35] Reviews of the game under its 1999 Edition title are covered on [[1999 - PGA Championship Golf 1999 Edition|the 1999 Edition page]]. In August 2000, Eurogamer reviewed the UK budget re-release, titled "PGA Championship Golf 1999", and called it "one of the top selling sports games of its day across the Atlantic".[^ref-15]
