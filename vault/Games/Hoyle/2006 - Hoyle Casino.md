# Hoyle Casino 2006

<small style="color: gray">Last updated: October 23, 2026</small>

## Overview

Hoyle Casino 2006 is a casino simulation game for Windows PC, released in the United States on September 27, 2005[^ref-1][^ref-2][^ref-3]. It was published by [[Encore Software]][^ref-1][^ref-6]; GameFAQs and Metacritic credit Sierra Entertainment as developer[^ref-1][^ref-3], while Wikipedia's series overview lists Encore as the series' developer and publisher from 2005 to 2016[^ref-4]. Giant Bomb and GameSpot likewise list Encore Software as publisher and Sierra as developer[^ref-21][^ref-24]. It belongs to the Hoyle Casino series, which began in 1996 as a spin-off of the Hoyle's Official Book of Games series[^ref-4]. GameFAQs' product description carries the Hoyle tagline "The official name in gaming"[^ref-16].

The game offers more than 500 variants of 16 popular casino games[^ref-5][^ref-18]. GameFAQs' product description highlights Texas Hold'em, slots and video poker, and table games, plus an official rulebook, and bills the package as "New for 2006"[^ref-16]. Encore's product page describes it as "set in a realistic Vegas environment"[^ref-18], and in October 2005 it was the featured product on Encore's homepage[^ref-19]. The ESRB rated it E for Everyone, with content descriptors for Mild Language and Simulated Gambling[^ref-6][^ref-16].

## Story Summary

As a casino simulation, Hoyle Casino 2006 has no narrative storyline. The Hoyle Casino series is set in a virtual Las Vegas casino in which players create profiles, receive a set amount of virtual money, and wager it at any part of the casino as they could in a real one[^ref-4]. IGN's summary of this edition notes the ability to customize characters as players explore its Vegas-style games[^ref-8].

## Gameplay

### Interface and Controls

Players move between the casino's games and wager virtual money rather than real stakes[^ref-4]. An official rulebook is included with the game[^ref-1][^ref-16]. Encore's product copy credits the game with "attention to rules, and a simple interface" aimed at both novice and expert computer users[^ref-18][^ref-20]. Detailed documentation of this edition's interface was not found in available sources.

The sources do not explain the gap between the stated 16 and the 20 named.

### Puzzles and Mechanics

Hoyle Casino 2006 has no traditional puzzles; play consists of the casino games listed above[^ref-5]. Texas Hold'em is among the games named in GameFAQs' product description[^ref-16] and heads Encore's own game list[^ref-18].

## Development

### Origins

The Hoyle Casino series originated in 1996 as a spin-off of the Hoyle's Official Book of Games series[^ref-4]. Wikipedia lists Sierra Entertainment as the series' developer and publisher from 1996 to 2003, and Encore as developer and publisher from 2005 to 2016, noting that publication moved to Encore after 2005[^ref-4]. In December 2004 Vivendi Universal Games divested its Hoyle-branded software products to Navarre Corp's Encore subsidiary, which took over sales, distribution and marketing of the Hoyle line[^ref-25]. Hoyle Casino 2006 was published by Encore Software[^ref-6][^ref-16], with GameFAQs, Metacritic and GameSpot crediting Sierra Entertainment as developer[^ref-1][^ref-3][^ref-21]. Encore's Hoyle site states that the Hoyle name and card designs are trademarks of the U.S. Playing Card Company, used under license by Encore[^ref-20].

### Production

No production history for Hoyle Casino 2006 was located. The retail release offered more than 500 variants of 16 casino games[^ref-5], and GameFAQs' product description bills it as "New for 2006"[^ref-16]. Encore sold the boxed version at a suggested list price of $19.99[^ref-18]. Encore's product page calls Hoyle Casino "the best-selling casino game of all time"; that is the publisher's marketing claim, not an independently verified figure[^ref-18].
