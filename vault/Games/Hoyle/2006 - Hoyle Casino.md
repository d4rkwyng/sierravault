# Hoyle Casino 2006

<small style="color: gray">Last updated: October 23, 2026</small>

## Overview

Hoyle Casino 2006 is a casino simulation game for Windows PC, released in the United States on September 27, 2005[^ref-1][^ref-2][^ref-3]. It was published by [[Encore Software]][^ref-1][^ref-6]; GameFAQs and Metacritic credit Sierra Entertainment as developer[^ref-1][^ref-3], while Wikipedia's series overview lists Encore as the series' developer and publisher from 2005 to 2016[^ref-4]. It belongs to the Hoyle Casino series, which began in 1996 as a spin-off of the Hoyle's Official Book of Games series[^ref-4]. The retail box carries the Hoyle tagline "The official name in gaming"[^ref-16].

The game offers more than 500 variants of 16 popular casino games[^ref-5]. The box description highlights Texas Hold'em, slots and video poker, and table games, plus an official rulebook, and bills the package as "New for 2006"[^ref-16]. The ESRB rated it E for Everyone, with content descriptors for Mild Language and Simulated Gambling[^ref-6][^ref-16].

## Story Summary

As a casino simulation, Hoyle Casino 2006 has no narrative storyline. The Hoyle Casino series is set in a virtual Las Vegas casino in which players create profiles, receive a set amount of virtual money, and wager it at any part of the casino as they could in a real one[^ref-4]. IGN's summary of this edition notes the ability to customize characters as players explore its Vegas-style games[^ref-8].
