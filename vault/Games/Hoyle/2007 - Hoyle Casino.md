# Hoyle Casino 2007

<small style="color: gray">Last updated: October 13, 2026</small>

## Overview

Hoyle Casino 2007 is a casino simulation game developed and published by [[Encore Software]] for Microsoft Windows, released in the United States on September 11, 2006[^ref-1][^ref-3]. According to its publisher description, it "offers over 500 variations of 16 of the most popular casino games"[^ref-3][^ref-4]. It belongs to the Hoyle Casino series, which began in 1996 as a spin-off of Sierra's Hoyle's Official Book of Games line and is set in a virtual Las Vegas casino where players wager virtual money[^ref-5][^ref-16].

Wikipedia credits Sierra with the Hoyle Casino games from 1996 to 2003 (Sierra in fact released editions as late as December 2004, before Vivendi sold the Hoyle line to Encore)[^ref-17][^ref-18]; Encore took over publishing the series from 2005 and continued it until 2016[^ref-5][^ref-16]. Wikipedia states that "the 2007 version" of Hoyle Casino suffered from a bug that froze card games for 30 seconds after each bet, which Encore could not fix and for which it offered refunds, but that statement has carried a "citation needed" tag since July 2009 and no other source for it has been found[^ref-5].

The game was rated T (Teen) by the ESRB for Simulated Gambling[^ref-1][^ref-3][^ref-7].

## Gameplay

### Structure

Hoyle Casino 2007 collects 16 casino games with over 500 rule variations[^ref-4]. As in the rest of the series, players create a profile, receive a set amount of virtual money, and can visit any part of the virtual casino to wager it as they would in a real casino[^ref-5].

### Games

The Amazon.ca product listing names Texas Hold 'Em, Baccarat, Big Raise Hold 'Em and Blackjack among the included games[^ref-7]. One Amazon customer's review refers to a Texas Hold 'Em tournament mode[^ref-7].

## Reception

### Contemporary Reviews

Metacritic lists no critic reviews and too few user ratings to produce a user score[^ref-3]. No contemporary press reviews have been found.

### User Feedback

Amazon.ca shows an average of 3.6 out of 5 stars from 10 ratings[^ref-7]. An Amazon customer gave it two stars, writing: "The games are great, but it keeps crashing in the middle of the Texas Hold Em tournament. The worst part is it does not save the game when it crashes."[^ref-7] Another Amazon customer, reviewing in 2021, wrote simply that it "Will not work with Windows 10"[^ref-7].

### Modern Assessment

The game has little modern presence: Grouvee records one member with it in their collection, one backlogging it and one wish-listing it, with no reviews[^ref-2].

## Development

### Origins

Hoyle Casino 2007 was one of Encore's annual Hoyle releases after the company took over publishing the series in 2005[^ref-5][^ref-6]. GameFAQs lists it as developed and published by Encore Software[^ref-1][^ref-6].

### Technical Issues

- **Reported freeze bug:** Wikipedia's uncited statement describes a 30-second freeze after each bet in card games in "the 2007 version", which Encore reportedly could not fix and for which customers could request a refund through the Encore USA website; Wikipedia says the bug was fixed in Hoyle Casino 2008[^ref-5]. Because the claim is uncited, and Wikipedia's edition list has no entry titled Hoyle Casino 2007 (its 2007 entry is "Hoyle Casino Games"), it is unclear which retail product it refers to[^ref-5].
- **Windows Vista:** A user on a Windows Vista newsgroup in September 2007 reported that the game's help button told him it did not work with Vista; another user suggested running setup as administrator or in Windows XP compatibility mode[^ref-9].
- **Tournament crashes:** An Amazon customer reported crashes during the Texas Hold 'Em tournament with no saved progress[^ref-7].
- **Windows 10:** An Amazon customer reported that it would not work with Windows 10[^ref-7].

## Legacy

### Sales and Commercial Impact

No sales figures for Hoyle Casino 2007 have been found. For the series as a whole, Hoyle Casino 2000 sold 230,365 units and earned $6.15 million in North America from January through October 2000, according to PC Data[^ref-5].

### Subsequent Releases

- **Hoyle Casino 2008:** Released in 2007 for PC and Macintosh, advertising "600+ classic games"[^ref-6]
- **Hoyle Casino 2009:** Released in 2008 for Macintosh and PC, described as "rebuilt from the ground up"[^ref-6]

Encore continued publishing Hoyle titles through 2016[^ref-5][^ref-16].
