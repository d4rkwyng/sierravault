# Hoyle Casino 2007

<small style="color: gray">Last updated: October 23, 2026</small>

## Overview

Hoyle Casino 2007 is a casino simulation game developed and published by [[Encore Software]] for Microsoft Windows. It was released in the United States in 2006; "2007" is part of the edition's name, not its release year. Sources disagree on the exact date: Amazon.com and IGN give August 8, 2006[^ref-20][^ref-26], while GameFAQs and Metacritic give September 11, 2006[^ref-1][^ref-3]. According to its publisher description, it "offers over 500 variations of 16 of the most popular casino games" and is "Set in a realistic Vegas environment"[^ref-3][^ref-4][^ref-19]. It belongs to the Hoyle Casino series, which began in 1996 as a spin-off of Sierra's Hoyle's Official Book of Games line and is set in a virtual Las Vegas casino where players wager virtual money[^ref-5][^ref-16].

Wikipedia credits Sierra with the Hoyle Casino games from 1996 to 2003 (Sierra in fact released editions as late as December 2004, before Vivendi sold the Hoyle line to Encore)[^ref-17][^ref-18]; Encore took over publishing the series from 2005 and continued it until 2016[^ref-5][^ref-16]. Wikipedia states that "the 2007 version" of Hoyle Casino suffered from a bug that froze card games for 30 seconds after each bet, which Encore could not fix and for which it offered refunds, but that statement has carried a "citation needed" tag since July 2009[^ref-5]. A moderator on Encore's own support forum did confirm in October 2007 that a long delay after craps dice were thrown was "a known problem with the 2007 version of Hoyle Casino" and that "there will be no fix for this problem"[^ref-22].

The game is rated T (Teen) by the ESRB, with the content descriptor Simulated Gambling[^ref-21].

## Gameplay

### Structure

Hoyle Casino 2007 collects 16 casino games with over 500 rule variations[^ref-4]. As in the rest of the series, players create a profile, receive a set amount of virtual money, and can visit any part of the virtual casino to wager it as they would in a real casino[^ref-5].

### Games

Encore's own product page lists the games as ONLINE Texas Hold 'Em, Baccarat, Blackjack, Caribbean Stud Poker, Craps, Fortune Pai-Gow Poker, Four Card Poker, Horse Racing, Keno, Let It Ride, Pai Gow Poker, Roulette, Royal Match 21, Slots, Video Poker, Three Card Poker, Video Blackjack, Solitaire and Spades[^ref-19]. That is 19 names, although the same copy describes "16 of the most popular casino games"[^ref-19]. The Amazon listings also name Big Raise Hold 'Em, and likewise bill Texas Hold 'Em as an online mode ("ONLINE Texas Hold 'Em")[^ref-7][^ref-20]. One Amazon customer's review refers to a Texas Hold 'Em tournament mode[^ref-7].

## Reception

Metacritic lists no critic reviews and too few user ratings to produce a user score[^ref-3]. No contemporary press reviews have been found.

Amazon.ca shows an average of 3.6 out of 5 stars from 10 ratings[^ref-7], and Amazon.com, listing the same product, shows 3.6 out of 5 from 20 global ratings[^ref-20]. ## Development

### Origins

Hoyle Casino 2007 was one of Encore's annual Hoyle releases after the company took over publishing the series in 2005[^ref-5][^ref-6]. GameFAQs lists it as developed and published by Encore Software[^ref-1][^ref-6]. Encore's Hoyle website sold it as a download for $19.99, with a "full featured, 60 minute trial", and the page carried the line "© 2007 Encore, Inc., A Navarre Corporation Company"; the Hoyle name and card designs were trademarks of the U.S. Playing Card Company "used under license by Encore, Inc."[^ref-19]

### Technical Issues

- **Craps delay:** In October 2007 a buyer on Encore's support forum reported that, on both Windows XP and Windows Vista, more than a minute passed after the craps dice were thrown before the results of the bets appeared. A forum moderator replied: "This is a known problem with the 2007 version of Hoyle Casino. Unfortunately, I'm afraid, there will be no fix for this problem."[^ref-22]
- **Wikipedia's freeze-bug account:** Wikipedia's uncited statement describes a 30-second freeze after each bet in card games in "the 2007 version", which Encore reportedly could not fix and for which customers could request a refund through the Encore USA website; Wikipedia says the bug was fixed in Hoyle Casino 2008[^ref-5]. Those specifics (card games, 30 seconds, refunds, the 2008 fix) remain unverified; the Encore forum confirms only the unfixed craps delay above[^ref-22]. Wikipedia's edition list calls the 2007 entry "Hoyle Casino Games"; IGN gives "Hoyle Casino Games (2007)" as an alternate name for this product, and Encore's forum titles its help file "Hoyle Casino Games 2007 Help", so the entry very likely refers to this game[^ref-5][^ref-24][^ref-26].
- **Windows Vista:** A user on a Windows Vista newsgroup in September 2007 reported that the game's help button told him it did not work with Vista; another user suggested running setup as administrator or in Windows XP compatibility mode[^ref-9]. A moderator on Encore's forum explained that the help (.hlp) files for the 2007 Hoyle products would not open on Windows Vista or Windows 7 because Microsoft had not included WinHelp32.exe in them, and posted the help files in PDF format instead[^ref-24].
- **Tournament crashes:** An Amazon customer reported crashes during the Texas Hold 'Em tournament with no saved progress[^ref-7]. A buyer on Encore's forum reported the game crashing during poker tournaments in December 2007; the moderator suggested disabling the background music, a workaround from an Encore knowledgebase article for Hoyle Casino 2006, while noting it was not known whether that fix would work for the 2007 edition[^ref-25].
- **Windows 10:** An Amazon customer reported that it would not work with Windows 10[^ref-7].

## Legacy

### Sales and Commercial Impact

No sales figures for Hoyle Casino 2007 have been found. For the series as a whole, Hoyle Casino 2000 sold 230,365 units and earned $6.15 million in North America from January through October 2000, according to PC Data[^ref-5]. Encore's marketing copy for this edition called HOYLE Casino "the best-selling casino game of all time"[^ref-19][^ref-20]; that is the publisher's own claim, and no independent figures supporting it have been found.

### Subsequent Releases

- **Hoyle Casino 2008:** Released in 2007 for PC and Macintosh, advertising "600+ classic games"[^ref-6]
- **Hoyle Casino 2009:** Released in 2008 for Macintosh and PC, described as "rebuilt from the ground up"[^ref-6]

Encore continued publishing Hoyle titles through 2016[^ref-5][^ref-16]. On May 21, 2013, Encore's customer service announced on its forum that technical support for Hoyle Casino 2007 was "no longer available from Encore Technical Support", although functionality support could still be obtained through the forums[^ref-23].
