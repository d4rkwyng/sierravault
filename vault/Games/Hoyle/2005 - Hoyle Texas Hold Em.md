# Hoyle Texas Hold 'Em 2005

<small style="color: gray">Last updated: October 23, 2026</small>

## Overview

Hoyle Texas Hold 'Em is a poker game for Windows, developed and published by Encore Software and released in the United States in February 2005[^ref-1][^ref-16]; GameSpot gives February 28, 2005[^ref-20]. Encore's own 2005 catalogue described it as teaching "the hottest game of poker according to the official rules with complex opponents and 14 different poker games to challenge all players," priced at $9.99[^ref-22]. The 14 games include Texas Hold'em, 5 Card Draw, 7 Card Stud and Omaha, with built-in tutorials[^ref-23]. Wikipedia lists it among the spin-offs of the Hoyle Casino series, dating it to 2006[^ref-4]; GameFAQs' release data gives February 2005 for the US release[^ref-16].

The game sits within the Hoyle brand, whose Hoyle Casino line was developed and published by Sierra Entertainment until publication moved to Encore after 2005[^ref-4]. No source found credits Sierra with any role in this title; GameFAQs and Metacritic both list Encore Software as developer[^ref-16][^ref-17].

## Story Summary

As a poker game, Hoyle Texas Hold 'Em has no narrative. Players sit at tables of talking computer opponents, whose portraits surround each table, and try to amass a fortune without going broke[^ref-18].

## Gameplay

### Interface and Controls

According to a reseller's product description (no second source has been found), opponents are a mix of characters, among them a bear, an Elvis impersonator and more "mundane" types[^ref-18]. The same description says players can create their own talking personas with a face-maker feature[^ref-18].

### Structure and Progression

The game offers 14 poker games in total, Texas Hold 'Em among them, along with Omaha, 7-Card Stud and 5-Card Draw[^ref-18][^ref-22][^ref-23]. Tutorials are available for each game type[^ref-18][^ref-23].

### Puzzles and Mechanics

A reseller's feature list for the game includes "Learn the official rules and master the game of Texas Hold'em"[^ref-18]. The ESRB rated it E for Everyone with the content descriptor "Simulated Gambling"[^ref-21], as GameFAQs also lists[^ref-16].

## Development

### Production

GameFAQs, GameSpot and Metacritic all credit Encore Software as developer and publisher[^ref-16][^ref-20][^ref-17]. Encore was then "A Navarre Corporation Company," per its 2005 catalogue[^ref-22]; Navarre Corporation had acquired Encore's assets out of bankruptcy court in August 2002[^ref-25]. No individual design credits have been found.

### Easter Eggs and Trivia

- The Hoyle brand is named for Edmond Hoyle (1672–1769), an English writer best known for his works on the rules and play of card games, the source of the phrase "according to Hoyle"[^ref-19]

## Legacy

### Collections

According to MobyGames, the Hoyle Friday Night Poker part of the later Hoyle Poker Series is based on Hoyle Texas Hold 'Em[^ref-2].

### Related Products

Hoyle Card Games 2005, released September 14, 2004, featured "more than 30 classic card games with in-game tutorials to help you become a better player"; Metacritic lists it as developed by Encore Software and published by VU Games[^ref-6].

Sierra's own earlier Hoyle Poker, released for Windows on April 30, 1997 and described by MobyGames as Sierra's "first foray into the world of poker," also included Texas Hold 'Em among its variations[^ref-24]; GameSpot counted "more than a dozen" and judged that "Sierra has done an admirable job of creating a sharp and faithful rendition of poker"[^ref-7].
