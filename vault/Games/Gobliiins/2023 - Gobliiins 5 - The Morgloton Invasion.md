# Gobliiins 5: The Morgloton Invasion

<small style="color: gray">Last updated: October 12, 2026</small>

## Overview

Gobliiins 5: The Morgloton Invasion (stylized as GOBLiiiNS5) is a 2D point-and-click adventure game released in 2023, the first new entry in the Gobliiins series since the 3D Gobliiins 4 of 2009[^ref-1][^ref-25]. It was made independently by [[Pierre Gilhodes]], the artist and co-creator behind the original Coktel Vision games, and self-published under his Schnibble Productions label; Coktel Vision itself had long since closed, and neither it nor Sierra had any part in this game[^ref-5][^ref-11][^ref-20]. Development was crowdfunded through campaigns on Ulule and Kickstarter in 2022[^ref-2][^ref-26]. The game brings back the three goblins of the 1991 original—Oups, Asgard, and Ignatius—on a mission to find the source of a magic ray that turns people into anthropomorphic potatoes[^ref-1].

Following the series' naming convention, the three 'i's in the title match the number of goblins the player controls[^ref-3]. The player directs one goblin at a time and switches between them, with each goblin bringing different abilities to the puzzles[^ref-1][^ref-5]. The game was first released on itch.io in May 2023 (May 2 per MobyGames) and on Steam on July 20, 2023[^ref-1][^ref-8][^ref-20]. Unlike the poorly received 3D Gobliiins 4, it returns to the series' 2D look, with hand-drawn art at 640×480[^ref-2][^ref-5].

The game was largely a solo effort, with Gilhodes handling the scenario, graphics, animation and programming in the Adventure Game Studio engine[^ref-8]. Fans on forums noted that he was not a programmer by trade[^ref-6]. As one itch.io commenter put it, "I honestly can't believe I got to play a new Gobliiins game after 30 years - very very happy!"[^ref-7]

## Story Summary

In the kingdom of King Angoulafre, the mad scientist Morglott plans to turn all of the king's subjects into potatoes with a mysterious magic ray[^ref-8][^ref-29]. Oups, Asgard, and Ignatius—the original trio from the first Gobliiins game—set out to find the source of the ray and stop him[^ref-1][^ref-8].

The adventure runs across sixteen levels in four parts[^ref-2][^ref-5]. Along the way the goblins visit Flatus Square, the king's chamber, a giant snail-boat, Morglot's kitchen and laboratory, and a greenhouse[^ref-14]. In Part 3 they enter Morglot's memories of his school days, where they deal with figures such as Ademard and Frippone and settle the grievances he has carried since his school days[^ref-14][^ref-16][^ref-25]. The last level, "The Wedding of the King," centres on a royal wedding: the goblins use an "unpotatotizer" to restore the potato-fied guests and finally King Angoulafre himself[^ref-14]. As is usual for the series, the plot mainly serves as a framework for the puzzles[^ref-11]. Riot Pixels found the story thin, with no coherent introduction or ending, and noted that the tale breaks off with a new global problem just as the main task is done[^ref-25].

## Gameplay

### Interface and Controls

Gobliiins 5 uses a classic point-and-click interface. The player controls one goblin at a time and switches between them; MobyGames classes it as 2D scrolling with a third-person, point-and-select interface[^ref-1][^ref-8]. Goblins are switched by clicking their portraits in the top-right corner, and the inventory bar at the top of the screen can get in the way when clicking on objects there[^ref-5][^ref-25]. Clicking a person or object gives a different result depending on which goblin is active[^ref-5][^ref-14].

A setup program, winsetup.exe, lets players choose French or English and switch between windowed and full-screen modes[^ref-10]. The game runs at a fixed 640×480 resolution[^ref-2][^ref-5].

### Structure and Progression

The game is divided into four parts of four levels each, for sixteen levels in total[^ref-2][^ref-5]. Each level is normally confined to a single scene that must be solved before the next one opens, a return to the level-based structure of the first Gobliiins[^ref-1][^ref-15]. The game ships as four separate executables, one for each part[^ref-2][^ref-10].

- **Part 1:** Opens in Flatus Square and the king's chamber; available as a free demo[^ref-14][^ref-16]
- **Part 2:** Begins aboard "The Snail"[^ref-14]
- **Part 3:** Morglot's kitchen, laboratory and memories; described by one forum player as the game's peak[^ref-14][^ref-16]
- **Part 4:** Begins in the greenhouse and ends with "The Wedding of the King"[^ref-14]

One forum player estimated 30 to 60 minutes per level and roughly 10 hours in total, similar to Gobliins 2[^ref-16]. Jefklak's Codex likewise counted "10 hours of head scratching"[^ref-5]. The game continues the series' tradition of no game over: failed experiments do not kill the goblins[^ref-1][^ref-25].

### Puzzles and Mechanics

In keeping with the series, Gobliiins 5's puzzles favour quirky "moon logic" over strict deduction. Jefklak's Codex advised, "Don't try to think too logically here," while judging the puzzles neither too easy nor too difficult[^ref-5]. The most characteristic puzzles require commands to be given to several goblins in quick succession[^ref-5].

Each goblin has a defined role. Oups does most of the talking and carries the inventory, Asgard punches things, and Ignatius casts spells with unpredictable effects[^ref-5][^ref-25]. Solving a level means combining item use with these character-specific actions[^ref-14].

One Steam reviewer found the puzzles "logical and not to difficult" [sic][^ref-17]. Riot Pixels, by contrast, called some puzzles baffling and the pixel-hunting relentless[^ref-25]. Some players also noted that certain items cannot be interacted with even though they look as if they can[^ref-16].

## Reception

### Contemporary Reviews

Gobliiins 5 was received warmly by long-time fans of the series. Riot Pixels (Oleg Zaitsev, November 11, 2023) gave it 65%. The review summed it up as, in translation, "a curiosity from a half-forgotten era. Even if its puzzles aren't always logical, a seasoned adventurer will hardly regret a few evenings spent in its company"[^ref-25]. It criticised the abrupt story, the lack of saving within levels, the sparse animation and the rough English translation[^ref-25].

Steam user reviews praised the game's faithfulness to the original trilogy. One reviewer wrote: "What an amazing game. It has the exact feel like the first three Gobliiins (haven't played 4 yet). It's funny, the puzzles are logical and not to difficult and the story is nice"[^ref-17]. Another wrote that it "feels unpolished on first glance but its a truly fun game and harks back to my childhood with GOBLIINS 3"[^ref-24]. On Steam, 92% of 70 user reviews are positive, and on itch.io the game is rated 4.9 out of 5 from 40 ratings[^ref-10][^ref-20].

### Modern Assessment

Jefklak's Codex rated the game "Good" and wrote that "Gobliiins 5 is clearly a diamond in the rough, especially compared to the 3D failure of Gobliins 4"[^ref-5]. The review faulted the stiff, unanimated backgrounds, the clumsy goblin switching and the inability to save, but praised the humour and the hand-drawn art[^ref-5].

A comment on Gobliiins 5's GOG.com wishlist page read: "This is a perfect match for GOG. It absolutely captures the feel and charm of the classic games and has amazingly detailed animation"[^ref-18]. RPG Codex forum users were more mixed. One called it "a picasso of adventure games. A labour of love from a true master"[^ref-16]. Another called it a "Pretty cool game, and while inferior to Gobs 2 and 3, it's little wonder given that this was only one guy working with an unfamiliar engine"[^ref-16].

**Scores:**
- **Riot Pixels:** 65%[^ref-25]
- **Steam:** 92% positive of 70 user reviews[^ref-20]
- **itch.io:** 4.9/5 from 40 user ratings[^ref-10]

## Development

### Origins

After the commercial and critical failure of Gobliiins 4, Pierre Gilhodes had given up on a fifth game[^ref-2]. Gilhodes, whose art defined the series and Coktel Vision's Woodruff and the Schnibble of Azimuth, regained his motivation after meeting Marion Poinsot, author of the comic *Le Donjon de Naheulbeuk*[^ref-1][^ref-2]. She persuaded him to revive the series, recommended the Adventure Game Studio engine, and helped him learn it and its English-language manual[^ref-2].

The project was crowdfunded in 2022, on Ulule in French and on Kickstarter in English[^ref-2]. The Ulule campaign ran from February 19 to March 21, 2022, and raised €6,950 against a €3,000 goal[^ref-26]. French Wikipedia puts the combined total at a little over €10,000, while Riot Pixels gives €14,000[^ref-2][^ref-25]. Gilhodes later said that he would have made the game even without the campaign, but that "it would have been hard financially" (translated from French)[^ref-2].

### Production

Development was largely a solo effort. Gilhodes is credited with the scenario, graphics, animations and programming, built in Adventure Game Studio (AGS)[^ref-8]. Forum observers noted that "Even though Pierre is not a programmer, he seems to be doing all the work with AGS"[^ref-6]. Unlike Gobliiins 4, the game returns to the series' 2D graphics, at 640×480[^ref-2].

Gilhodes's art style stayed recognisable. One forum player laughed at "how much the guy's artstyle remained almost exactly the same over the years in some aspects"[^ref-16]. Reviewers praised the hand-drawn art and character animation but noted the lack of background animation[^ref-5][^ref-25].

**Development Credits (Windows):**[^ref-8]
- **Scenario, Graphics, Animations, Programming:** Pierre Gilhodes
- **Music:** Philipp Alexander Marguerre, Christian Laly, Thierry Teodori, Didier Sallustro
- **English translation:** Carole Corvisy, Matt Giuca
- **Thanks for her invaluable help:** Marion Poinsot

### Technical Notes

The game runs at a fixed 640×480 resolution with 2D hand-drawn art[^ref-2][^ref-5]. Reviewers found the animation sparse, often only a few frames per action, and the scrolling choppy[^ref-5][^ref-25]. Because it is an AGS game, it can also be run through ScummVM[^ref-1].

### Technical Issues

Because the game is split into four executables, finishing Part 1 does not lead straight into Part 2. The Steam store page warns that once the King's Chamber is finished, players must exit and restart the game in Steam to launch the next part[^ref-20]. Players who missed this saw an "unresolved import 'ShellExecute'" error message at that point[^ref-20].

Screen flickering in full-screen mode was reported by a player using an adaptive-sync (G-Sync) display. It went away when adaptive sync was turned off[^ref-7]. Saving is only possible between levels[^ref-5][^ref-25]. An F5 quick-save worked in an early build but was removed in a later version[^ref-7]. Gilhodes has since said he plans a new single-part version with quick saving[^ref-10]. Players also noted that some interactive elements were hard to tell apart from the background[^ref-16].

Gilhodes released post-launch updates, for example fixing a major bug in Part 3's kitchen and a minor one in Morglot's room[^ref-7].

### Easter Eggs and Trivia

- The number of 'i's in each Gobliiins title matches the number of playable goblins, so this fifth game, with three goblins, has three 'i's[^ref-3]
- Gilhodes self-publishes under the name Schnibble Productions, which shares the word "Schnibble" with his earlier Coktel game Woodruff and the Schnibble of Azimuth[^ref-1][^ref-20]
- The first part of the game is a free download, serving both as a demo and as the full first quarter of the game[^ref-8][^ref-16]
- After the game's release, Gilhodes announced a sixth game, Gobliins 6, starring Fingus and Winkle from Gobliins 2; it was released in February 2026[^ref-1][^ref-2][^ref-28]

## Voice Cast

There is no conventional voice acting and no voice cast is credited. Dialogue appears as on-screen text, while the characters "speak" in comic, unintelligible gibberish[^ref-8][^ref-25].

## Legacy

### Sales and Commercial Impact

Gobliiins 5's release was low-key. According to the French magazine JV, it mainly reached people who already knew about the crowdfunding campaign. JV added (translated from French): "It would be a lie to say it caused a commercial tidal wave on Steam. It does, however, seem rather well received and enjoyed as a return to childhood, whether in France or across the Atlantic"[^ref-2].

The crowdfunding campaigns showed that interest in the Gobliiins series continued decades after the original games[^ref-2][^ref-26]. Fans also voted for the game on GOG.com's wishlist, asking for it to be sold there[^ref-18].

### Collections

In May 2026, Red Art Games released the Gobliiins Collection for PlayStation 5, Xbox Series and Switch. It brings together the first five games, including Gobliiins 5, and a physical PS5 edition was announced for September[^ref-29]. On PC, Gobliiins 5 is sold on Steam and itch.io[^ref-10][^ref-20]. GOG.com sells only the Gobliiins pack of the first three games, and Gobliiins 5 is not on GOG[^ref-18][^ref-27].

### Fan Projects

Because the game was built in Adventure Game Studio, it can be played through ScummVM on platforms beyond Windows, including macOS, Linux, Android and iOS[^ref-1]. A fan made an unofficial Mac port of the demo using a modified ScummVM 2.7.0. It ran on Intel Macs with macOS 10.13 and later, and the porter hoped to add Mac OS X 10.9, Apple Silicon and PowerPC builds later[^ref-21]. He wrote: "I love all platforms equally and if I ever have the luck of being hired as a ports wizard in an official capacity... I'll do my darndest to deliver really optimized experiences for everybody"[^ref-21].

### Related Publications

Community-created resources emerged to support players:
- **Walkthrough King Guide:** Text walkthrough covering all four parts, updated January 2024[^ref-14]

### Critical Perspective

Gobliiins 5 is an unusual case: a genuine sequel to a classic series, made by its original artist three decades after the first game and funded directly by fans[^ref-2][^ref-26]. It shows both what solo independent development can achieve and where it falls short. Reviewers credited its humour and its Gobliins feel while pointing to rough edges such as clumsy controls, no saving within levels and sparse animation[^ref-5][^ref-25]. Players found the quality uneven from part to part[^ref-16].

Its return to 2D after the poorly received 3D Gobliiins 4 was welcomed by reviewers[^ref-5][^ref-25]. Gilhodes followed it with Gobliins 6 in February 2026[^ref-1][^ref-28].
