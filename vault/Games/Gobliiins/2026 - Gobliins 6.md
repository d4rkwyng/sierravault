# Gobliins 6: The Madmen of the Year 1000

<small style="color: gray">Last updated: October 26, 2026</small>

## Overview

*Gobliins 6: The Madmen of the Year 1000* (styled *GOBLiiNS6 - The Prince Buffoon - The Madmen of the Year 1000*) is a 2026 2D point-and-click puzzle adventure that serves as a direct sequel to *[[1992 - Gobliins 2 - The Prince Buffoon|Gobliins 2: The Prince Buffoon]]*, reuniting the duo Fingus and Winkle more than thirty years after their original adventure.[^ref-1][^ref-2][^ref-14][^ref-18][^ref-20] Italian site Multiplayer.it described it as the official follow-up to both *Gobliiins 5* and *Gobliins 2*.[^ref-26] Developed by [[Pierre Gilhodes]] and published under his [[Schnibble Productions]] label, it is, in the words of its store page, "a new episode of the series GOBLiiiNS, directed by the initial author" — Gilhodes having created the visual look of the series and its characters at Coktel Vision in the early 1990s.[^ref-4][^ref-14][^ref-24]

The game was crowdfunded in parallel on Ulule (from October 9, 2024) and Kickstarter (October 10 to November 9, 2024); the Kickstarter closed with €9,524 from 388 backers against a €4,000 goal.[^ref-1][^ref-9][^ref-25] It was released for Windows on itch.io on February 10, 2026, and on Steam on February 13, 2026.[^ref-14][^ref-15][^ref-27] Like *Gobliiins 5*, it was built with Adventure Game Studio (AGS).[^ref-4][^ref-20]

Following the series naming convention, the number of letter "i"s in the title indicates the number of goblins the player controls, and the *GOBLiiNS6* title has two, for Fingus and Winkle.[^ref-4] The title count is not the whole story: according to MobyGames, Prince Buffoon becomes a third playable character later in the game, once he is found.[^ref-24] On the campaign page, Gilhodes wrote that "many players are very fond of Fingus and Winkle in Gobliins2, I hope they will be happy to find their new adventures in Gobliins6."[^ref-1]

## Story Summary

The story is set years after *Gobliins 2*, in which Fingus and Winkle were sent to rescue King Angoulafre's son, the Prince Buffoon, who had been kidnapped by the evil Amoniak.[^ref-4] In *Gobliins 6* the goblins once again set out "in search of king Angoulafre's son, Prince Buffoon".[^ref-14][^ref-20]

The campaign describes **Fingus** as "a lonely and depressed beekeeper, without news of his sidekick Winkle"; Adventure Gamers' announcement called him "a lonely and somewhat downcast beekeeper" who is "thrust back into action when he learns that his old friend Winkle is missing".[^ref-1][^ref-2] MobyGames' summary has Fingus, settled in the countryside, receive a message from the King asking for help; despite the King's wish that Winkle stay out of it, Fingus travels to the city to find his friend and the two set off together.[^ref-24]

The setting is "a troubled time for the people of King Angoulafre": the approach of the new millennium "exacerbates behavior" and war is at the gates of the kingdom. The goblins must save the world from "the cruel conqueror Karcassio".[^ref-1] MobyGames adds that the King's new royal mage has become a very influential figure and that a cult is rapidly gaining followers.[^ref-24] The campaign text notes that "several main objectives motivate our characters and as in the past, they are looking for the son of King Angoulafre, the Prince Buffoon."[^ref-1]

## Gameplay

### Interface and Controls

*Gobliins 6* is a single-player, mouse-driven point-and-click adventure in 2D, drawn in "large scenery in 16/9th".[^ref-14][^ref-18][^ref-20] It ships as a single executable, and the language (English or French) can be changed "directly without leaving the application".[^ref-1][^ref-14] Display options such as full-screen or windowed mode are set through the separate `winsetup.exe` file.[^ref-14][^ref-20]

### Structure and Progression

The game spans 16 levels "packed with puzzles, characters, music and humor", and progress is saved automatically at the start of each level; players can also save at any time.[^ref-1][^ref-20][^ref-24] Gilhodes's April 2025 progress report described level 13 as "a level on the edge of a lake that has some quietness, centered on the Buffoon", level 14 as an underwater level, and level 15 as the penultimate one.[^ref-28] Later patch notes name level 14 "Les profondeurs, Le grand rêveur" (The Depths, The Dreamer) and the second-to-last level the Castle of Sombrague.[^ref-22]

### Puzzles and Mechanics

The store description notes that "the characters are faced with numerous puzzles, they work in cooperation and each has their inventory."[^ref-14][^ref-20] Players switch between Fingus and Winkle, the pair carried over from *Gobliins 2*; there, Wikipedia describes Fingus as "well-mannered, intelligent, and shy" and Winkle as "strong, obnoxious, but dimwitted", noting that both can pick up and use items but generally use an item in different ways.[^ref-4] Once Prince Buffoon is found, he joins them as a third playable character.[^ref-24]

The Kickstarter campaign lists these improvements over *Gobliiins 5*:[^ref-1][^ref-2]

- The game is in 16:9, "an extended display"[^ref-1]
- "More game amplitude also means more puzzles, characters and funny situations"[^ref-1]
- A single executable with in-game language selection[^ref-1][^ref-14]
- An enriched save system: automatic saving at each level, plus saving progress at any time[^ref-1][^ref-2]
- Each of the two main characters has their own inventory[^ref-1][^ref-2]

## Reception

### Contemporary Reviews

As of October 2026, searches turned up no professional critic reviews of *Gobliins 6*, and it has no aggregate critic score. Press coverage so far has been news rather than reviews: Adventure Gamers and Multiplayer.it reported the crowdfunding launch in October 2024, and Indie Retro News covered the itch.io launch on February 10, 2026, reporting that players could "download and play" it from that day.[^ref-2][^ref-15][^ref-26]

### Modern Assessment

Reception so far comes from player ratings. On Steam, user reviews are rated "Positive", with 87% of 24 reviews positive.[^ref-14] On itch.io the game is rated 5.0 out of 5 from 17 ratings.[^ref-20] One itch.io player called it "definitely the second best game in the series, next to Goblins3," praising the "humor, graphics, and animation" and adding that "the music is much better compared to Gobliiins5."[^ref-23]

The project was discussed across several adventure-gaming communities:

- **RPG Codex** reposted the campaign launch and successive Kickstarter updates; early reactions included "Looks absolutely fantastic", and one user wrote: "I love it the guy just keeps doing his own thing, screw the current 'trends'."[^ref-9]
- **Adventure-Treff** (German) posted the Ulule launch on October 9, 2024, with an English translation of the campaign text.[^ref-10]
- **GOG.com forums** carried a thread titled "Gobliins 6 - Gobliins 2 sequel to Kickstart", linking to the campaign.[^ref-7]
- **SteamGifts** hosted a thread that reprinted the Adventure Gamers announcement and was later retitled for the February 13 Steam release.[^ref-8]

## Development

### Origins

According to the Kickstarter campaign, work on the game had "been underway since April 2023", the year *Gobliiins 5* was released.[^ref-1][^ref-4] Multiplayer.it reported that the good sales of *Gobliiins 5* encouraged Gilhodes to try a new episode.[^ref-26]

### Crowdfunding

Crowdfunding ran on two platforms at once. The Ulule campaign started on October 9, 2024 and closed on November 13, 2024 with €5,040 from 154 supporters against a €4,000 goal (126%).[^ref-25][^ref-10] The Kickstarter campaign ran from October 10 to November 9, 2024, also with a €4,000 goal.[^ref-1] In an October 29, 2024 update, Gilhodes referred to "the funding difficulties on both platforms".[^ref-29][^ref-9]

At its close the Kickstarter had raised €9,524 from 388 backers, 238% of the goal, according to the funding banner RPG Codex reposted.[^ref-9] Late pledges have since raised the total shown on the campaign page to €11,448 from 470 backers.[^ref-1]

According to the campaign page, the money would "finance production, which is expected to last approximately two and a half years", and pay "the main musician, Butterfly Tea". Depending on resources, Gilhodes hoped to bring in a graphic designer to help with animations, and testers would also be paid.[^ref-1][^ref-2]

### Production

The campaign's "Risks and challenges" section stated that the game, begun in April 2023, "will be finished in a year or a year and a half"; the base reward's estimated delivery was December 2025.[^ref-1] In an April 6, 2025 Kickstarter update, Gilhodes reported: "Although 75% of the game is almost finished, the last quarter started well, there is still a lot of work to do." The same update said the English translation by Carole was "moving slowly".[^ref-28][^ref-9] MobyGames credits Gilhodes with the scenario, graphics, animation and programming, Carole Corvisy with the English translation, Claire Taupin as lead tester, and Butterfly Tea Music for the music.[^ref-6]

In Kickstarter Update #8 on February 2, 2026, Gilhodes wrote: "As soon as the game is released, and as soon as I have collected the keys, I start sending you the emails. I send a Steam key by default, but let me know quickly if you prefer an Itch.io key, or the game files."[^ref-13] The itch.io release was briefly held up by the file size; in a comment on the store page Gilhodes wrote: "I am waiting for a response from Itch.io to release the game. The executable is too heavy, they must add more space."[^ref-27]

The game went on sale on itch.io on February 10, 2026.[^ref-15][^ref-27] In Kickstarter Update #9 on February 11, Gilhodes wrote: "Hello, all mailings are done, check your spam emails. Let me know in case of any problem. The game should be on Steam on Friday."[^ref-21] The Steam release followed on Friday, February 13, 2026, announced to backers in Update #11, "Le jeu est sorti sur Steam".[^ref-14][^ref-22]

### Technical Achievements

*Gobliins 6* is made with Adventure Game Studio, an open-source engine for graphic adventure games, and Clip Studio Paint, per its itch.io listing.[^ref-19][^ref-20] Gilhodes used AGS again after *Gobliiins 5*.[^ref-4] The developer's itch.io listing states that no generative AI was used.[^ref-20]

## Legacy

### Continuation of a Classic Franchise

*Gobliins 6* continues a series that began at Coktel Vision in 1991, later published by Sierra On-Line.[^ref-4] The original *Gobliiins* had the player coordinate three goblins with different abilities; later entries varied the number of playable characters.[^ref-4]

### Series Timeline

Wikipedia counts six games in the series. The "Goblins" column follows the "i" count in each title:[^ref-4]

| Year | Title | Goblins | Developer / Publisher |
|------|-------|---------|-----------|
| 1991 | Gobliiins | 3 | Coktel Vision |
| 1992 | Gobliins 2: The Prince Buffoon | 2 | Coktel Vision |
| 1993 | Goblins Quest 3 | 1 | Coktel Vision |
| 2009 | Gobliiins 4 | 3 | Société Pollene / Snowball Studios |
| 2023 | Gobliiins 5: The Morgloton Invasion | 3 | Schnibble Productions |
| 2026 | Gobliins 6: The Madmen of the Year 1000 | 2 (Prince Buffoon joins later)[^ref-24] | Schnibble Productions |

### Pierre Gilhodes' Role

Gilhodes created the visual look of the series and its characters at Coktel Vision, and his style was also used in *The Bizarre Adventures of Woodruff and the Schnibble*.[^ref-4] *Gobliiins 4* (2009) was designed by Gilhodes and developed by Société Pollene; [[Muriel Tramis]] has only a thanks credit, as "co-author of the scenarios for the first three Goblins and the ergonomics system used in Gobliiins 4."[^ref-30] With *Gobliiins 5* and *Gobliins 6* he has continued the series independently, self-publishing on itch.io and Steam under the Schnibble Productions name.[^ref-5][^ref-14][^ref-24]
