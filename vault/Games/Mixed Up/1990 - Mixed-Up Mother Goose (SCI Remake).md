# Mixed-Up Mother Goose

<small style="color: gray">Last updated: October 13, 2026</small>

## Overview

Mixed-Up Mother Goose (SCI Remake) is the first of three remakes Sierra On-Line made of Roberta Williams' 1987 children's adventure *Mixed-Up Mother Goose*[^ref-7]. Wikipedia calls it "Mixed-Up Mother Goose Enhanced" and says it "was remade using the SCI0 system, which meant improved graphics"[^ref-7]. MobyGames describes it as an updated version that "utilizes Sierra's SCI game interpreter and features enhanced graphics and sound," while "the gameplay remains the same as the previous version"[^ref-19].

Wikipedia dates the remake to 1990[^ref-7]; MobyGames lists its DOS, Amiga, Apple II and Apple IIgs releases under 1991[^ref-19]. It is a different game from the 1991 VGA/CD remake, which added digitized speech and VGA art[^ref-7][^ref-17].

## Story Summary

The premise is unchanged from the original: "all of the nursery rhymes in Mother Goose Land have become mixed up!"[^ref-19] The player finds the missing items for each nursery-rhyme character. MobyGames gives two examples: "Little Bo Peep is looking for her sheep and Jack Be Nimble lost his candle stick"[^ref-19]. When an item is returned, "the complete nursery rhyme will play with the lyrics appearing on screen"[^ref-19].

## Gameplay

Wikipedia says the game has "18 nursery rhymes to choose from, 20 items to recover"[^ref-7]. Old King Cole needs three items. Only the 1991 VGA remake cut his requirement to one, leaving eighteen items[^ref-7]. The player picks one of eight characters at the start[^ref-7].

The player "can only carry one item at a time," and "each time the game is played objects will be in a new location"[^ref-19][^ref-5]. A map of Mother Goose Land was included with the game[^ref-19][^ref-5].

Adventure Gamers called it "a children's adventure game so simple that once you start the game, even a young child can take over"[^ref-3].

## Development

MobyGames credits this version (DOS) as follows[^ref-19]:
- Game designer: Roberta Williams
- Executive producer: Ken Williams
- Producer: Josh Mandel
- Lead programmer: Juan Carlos Escobar
- Art designer: Gerald Moore
- Composer: James William Elliot
- "Mother Goose" theme: Amenda Lombardo
- Music arrangements and sound effects: Ken Allen
- Documentation: Marti McKenna

### Atari ST upgrade

Wikipedia says a "Classic's Reillustrated" (SCI EGA) version was "planned and announced" for the Atari ST in *Sierra News Magazine* (Spring 1991)[^ref-7]. Owners could send in disk #1 or the manual cover with $15 to upgrade, and "a retail boxed version was not sold in stores"[^ref-7].

## Legacy

This version was followed by the 1991 VGA/CD remake and by *Mixed-Up Mother Goose Deluxe* (1995)[^ref-7]. The *Roberta Williams Anthology* includes the 1987 original and the 1991 multimedia CD remake, but not this SCI0 version[^ref-7].
