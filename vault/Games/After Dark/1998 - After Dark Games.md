# After Dark Games

<small style="color: gray">Last updated: November 12, 2026</small>

## Overview

After Dark Games represents a unique entry in Sierra's catalog - a collection of eleven mini-games based on the beloved After Dark screensaver series.[^ref-1] Released for Windows on October 29, 1998[^ref-8][^ref-34] by Sierra Attractions and [[Berkeley Systems]][^ref-1][^ref-3], this CD-ROM compilation transformed iconic screensaver characters into interactive gaming experiences for both Windows 95 and Macintosh platforms.[^ref-3] The collection featured games modeled after previously released screensavers from the After Dark series[^ref-1], including the famous Flying Toasters, Bad Dog, and Hula Girl characters.[^ref-1][^ref-31]

J.C. Herz of The New York Times called it "a candy box of games and puzzles" and wrote that "these vest-pocket video games are, in their own way, as engaging and addictive as the mega-selling 40-hour sagas with souped-up 3-D sound and state-of-the-art computer graphics. Part of their appeal is sheer simplicity."[^ref-31] Computer Gaming World judged it "an interesting collection that will appeal to a wide audience."[^ref-32] Jack Eastman, who had co-created the screensaver, later said Berkeley Systems' philosophy "was to remain 'aggressively stupid'".[^ref-7]

## Story Summary

Rather than featuring a single narrative, After Dark Games presents eleven individual games, a spin-off from the successful After Dark screensaver collections.[^ref-11] The games range from arcade-style action to puzzles that test word and problem-solving skills.[^ref-12]

Characters like the Flying Toasters, which Jack Eastman conceived while hunting for something "really fun" for After Dark 2.0, when the kitchen toaster caught his eye and "my sleep-deprived brain put wings on it"[^ref-7], became the stars of their own gaming adventures. The compilation ranges from word games (Fish Shtick and Bad Dog 911) to arcade games (Hula Girl, Rodger Dodger and Mowin' Maniac).[^ref-17]

## Gameplay

### Interface and Controls

One review notes that "the interface couldn't be simpler, with rules provided online for each of the titles."[^ref-14] Each mini-game utilizes standard keyboard and mouse inputs[^ref-10], with "no complicated set-up" required - players can "just start playing."[^ref-8]

### The Eleven Games

After Dark Games includes the following titles, each with "multiple levels of difficulty to challenge any player"[^ref-8]:

| Game | Type | Description |
|------|------|-------------|
| **Toaster Run** | Arcade | Isometric Glider clone featuring Flying Toaster and Super Guy; "Don't get burnt in the high-flying arcade action"[^ref-1][^ref-8] |
| **Bad Dog 911** | Word Puzzle | Word scramble based on Bad Dog modules[^ref-1] |
| **Mowin' Maniac** | Action | "Go crazy with Mowin' Man and turn some pesky gardeners into fertilizer"[^ref-8] |
| **Hula Girl** | Platformer | Endless 2D platforming game based on the "Hula Twins" screensaver module[^ref-1] |
| **Roof Rats** | Puzzle | Similar to SameGame; match and clear colored tiles[^ref-1]. GameSpot calls it "a Tetris-type game"[^ref-17], and Computer Gaming World describes it as a challenge "to eliminate colored rooms in order to rescue twisted tenants"[^ref-32] |
| **Zapper** | Trivia | Knowledge-based trivia game[^ref-1] |
| **Fish Shtick** | Word Puzzle | Aquatic-themed word scramble[^ref-1] |
| **Foggy Boxes** | Strategy | Classic dots and boxes game[^ref-1] |
| **MooShu Tiles** | Puzzle | Mahjong-style tile matching game[^ref-1] |
| **Rodger Dodger** | Arcade | Clone of the game Raimais, according to Wikipedia[^ref-1]. GameSpot and MobyGames spell the title "Roger Dodger"[^ref-17][^ref-18] |
| **Solitaire** | Card | Classic solitaire; Computer Gaming World noted it was the only one of the eleven that "appears without some sort of mutilation" by the After Dark motif[^ref-32] |

The collection also includes "a free After Dark Games screen saver" as a bonus feature.[^ref-8]

### Puzzles and Mechanics

GameSpot granted that "the developers took time to produce sharp, clear graphics, and each game has original music," though "the short music bites loop endlessly."[^ref-17] GameFAQs' marketing copy bills Toaster Run as "high-flying arcade action"[^ref-8], while other games offer varied challenges from puzzle-solving to arcade reflexes.

One reviewer spent "two hours after I first popped the After Dark Games CD into my computer I was still sitting there, mesmerized."[^ref-12] Herz noted the games "all of which take less than 10 minutes" are "miniature experiences, simple to pick up and put down."[^ref-31]

Computer Gaming World listed the price as $29.95 and the publisher as "Berkeley/Sierra On-Line". Baker named Roof Rats, Toaster Run and Foggy Boxes (which he called "a tic-tac-toe relative") as the "stand-out games," each "proving addictive in its own way."[^ref-32]

GameSpot's review, published on April 29, 2000, a year after Computer Gaming World's and the New York Times's, was critical.[^ref-17] Freeman noted "games that just don't bring any life to the desktop puzzle genre" and that "each game is either a blatant rip-off of better-known games or is just so close, the only puzzle to solve will be trying to figure out what game it reminds you of."[^ref-17]

### Modern Assessment

MobyGames player reviews have been positive:
- "After Dark Games is a collection of eleven entertaining games with great sound and graphics. A few of the games are clones of popular games, but most of them are original." - Katakis[^ref-18]
- "A small wonder-package, which contains all those games which quickly de-stress a wound up person." - M. van Hilten[^ref-18]
- "I would say...'I know a great game package you can get for your kids...but you should not tell them that you have it for a couple of months while you play all the games!!!'" - Renee RAsul[^ref-18]

However, compatibility issues have emerged with modern systems, and in 2007 one Apple Discussions poster wrote that they had read that "the new intel processor will not recognize these old games."[^ref-20]

## Development

### Origins

After Dark Games emerged from the success of Berkeley Systems' screensaver empire, which began as creator Jack Eastman's personal project at Lawrence Berkeley Laboratory in 1986.[^ref-7] The Flying Toasters concept that became central to the collection came while Eastman was looking for "something really fun" for After Dark 2.0: "My sleep-deprived brain put wings on it."[^ref-7]

Eastman said Berkeley Systems' philosophy was to remain "aggressively stupid"[^ref-7], and when asked what made After Dark unique, he answered: "Charm; creativity; non-repetitiveness; stupidity."[^ref-7]

The original After Dark software was created by Jack Eastman and Patrick Beard; Eastman wrote the early code in assembly language, "the only way to hack the OS calls in those days."[^ref-7] After Dark for Windows came from a collaboration with Bill Stewart and Ian MacDonald of Software Dynamics.[^ref-7]

### Production

By the time of After Dark Games, Berkeley Systems belonged to CUC International, which acquired it in April 1997, the year after CUC bought Sierra On-Line.[^ref-30] Wikipedia describes the buyer as CUC's Sierra On-Line division.[^ref-33] The game was published under the Sierra Attractions label.[^ref-29]

Eastman returned as a consultant: "I worked on After Dark Games as a consultant. I was sort of Alpha Geek for that project."[^ref-7] MobyGames credits him as System Architect (Eightfold Way Consultants), with Phil Mikkelson as Game Design Consultant, Jane Scolieri (Olive Productions) as composer and sound designer, and Nick Rush as Creative Director.[^ref-13] Each game has its own original music.[^ref-17]

### Technical Achievements

After Dark Games turned screensaver characters (Flying Toasters, Bad Dog, Mowin' Man, Hula Girl and others) into eleven playable games plus a bonus screen saver.[^ref-32] Computer Gaming World found the graphics "amusing and well executed" but small, noting "the games only run at 640x480 in 256 colors"; at higher resolutions they run in windows. It called the bundled screen saver "merely a repetitive collection of short demonstrations of the different games."[^ref-32]

Screensaver compatibility note (this concerns the After Dark screensaver, not After Dark Games): 32-bit screensavers (After Dark 4.0) work on Windows 10/11, while 16-bit versions do not.[^ref-22]

## Legacy

### Cultural Impact

The Berkeley Systems founders, Joan Blades and Wes Boyd, went on to found MoveOn.org in 1998.[^ref-33] The Bad Dog screensaver character also inspired an animated TV series, first broadcast by Teletoon on March 1, 1999.[^ref-1]

### Preservation Efforts

GOG wishlist requests demonstrate ongoing preservation interest, with users sharing memories: "This was one of the few games that my mother had back when I was in elementary school on our windows 2000 pc; we'd play it together at times and it's just a very simple, but cozy anthology of games that would be very nice to have preserved."[^ref-25]

Another user noted: "I'm not even here for the games. I am however, here for the massive trove/suite of screensavers/modules produced over by Berkley. To even have them running in a little window themselves would be a fascinating way to recapture a major stone of screensaver history."[^ref-25]

In August 2026 developer Jeff Halter released Afterglow, which he describes as "an emulator built specifically for running After Dark modules -- the original 68k module code, not a remake or port -- with no dependencies on ROMs or classic Mac OS". It runs the screensaver modules on modern macOS rather than the games.[^ref-37]

### Speedrunning

Speedrun.com hosts a small set of leaderboards for several games in the collection.[^ref-19] Players compete in categories including "Toaster Run - Easy" with recorded times and verification.[^ref-26]

### Collections

After Dark Games was also packaged in other Sierra releases: After Dark Midnight Collection, Hoyle Casino 3 (1998), Hoyle Card Games 3 (1999), Hoyle Casino 4 (1999) and Hoyle Word Games (1999).[^ref-29] A demo (7.08 MB) offers five free rounds of Roof Rats, Bad Dog and Mowin' Maniac.[^ref-29]
