# Himalaya Studios

<small style="color: gray">Last updated: October 16, 2026</small>

## Overview

Himalaya Studios is an independent developer of adventure games. It was established as a limited liability company in 2004 in Chandler, Arizona, by Britney K. Brimhall and Christopher T. Warren, and was incorporated as a C corporation in Delaware in 2011.[^ref-10] MobyGames gives the same founding year, place and founders.[^ref-2] Wikipedia describes it as making adventure games "in the spirit of classic Sierra adventure games, like King's Quest, Space Quest, and Leisure Suit Larry."[^ref-10]

Brimhall and Warren had earlier founded [[AGD Interactive]], the non-profit group known for its free remakes of Sierra adventure games.[^ref-10] Himalaya is their commercial company: in 2006 Adventure Classic Gaming described it as "a for-profit game creation company."[^ref-22]

## History

### Founding

In a 2006 interview, Adventure Classic Gaming noted that Brimhall co-founded Himalaya Studios with Warren in 2004, and described her as "Chairman and CEO of Himalaya Studios."[^ref-22] Brimhall said that she moved to Australia "to work with AGD Interactive cofounder in creating our first original title," bringing the early work on Al Emmo that she had begun while living in Germany.[^ref-22]

### Al Emmo and the Lost Dutchman's Mine (2006)

The studio's first game, Al Emmo and the Lost Dutchman's Mine, was released on September 5, 2006.[^ref-10] It was built with Chris Jones' Adventure Game Studio.[^ref-21]

### Mage's Initiation (2013–2019)

Himalaya's second game, Mage's Initiation: Reign of the Elements, was crowdfunded on Kickstarter in early 2013 and raised about $125,000.[^ref-3] It was released in 2019 and received an aggregated Metacritic score of 70.[^ref-10]

## Notable Games

### Al Emmo and the Lost Dutchman's Mine (2006)

Al Emmo is a 42-year-old man who still lives with his parents. He finds a wife through the Mail Order Monthly magazine and "heads west by train from the New Yawk Central to Anozira," a Wild West town whose name is Arizona spelled backwards, only for the marriage plans to collapse when he arrives.[^ref-21] The game runs at a single resolution of 640x400, and Adventure Classic Gaming notes that its "hand drawn graphics lend the game a distinct retro style"; a 2010 update added new high-resolution graphics.[^ref-21] See the [[2006 - Al Emmo and the Lost Dutchman's Mine|Al Emmo]] page for more.

### Mage's Initiation: Reign of the Elements (2019)

Mage's Initiation is a point-and-click adventure and RPG hybrid set in the land of Iginor. "In the tradition of Sierra's classic 'Quest for Glory' series," the player chooses one of four character classes: Fire, Earth, Air or Water Mage.[^ref-18] See the [[2019 - Mage's Initiation - Reign of the Elements|Mage's Initiation]] page for more.
