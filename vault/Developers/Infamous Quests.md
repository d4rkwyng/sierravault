# Infamous Quests

<small style="color: gray">Last updated: October 26, 2026</small>

## Overview

Infamous Quests is an independent adventure game developer based in Syracuse, New York. Steven Alexander and Shawn Mills founded it in 2012.[^ref-3][^ref-9] The two had earlier run [[Infamous Adventures]], an amateur studio that made free remakes of Sierra adventures. They set up Infamous Quests "in order to separate it from their free fan-remake development company".[^ref-9] Its games are *Quest for Infamy* (2014), inspired by Sierra's Quest for Glory series, and *Order of the Thorne: The King's Challenge* (2016).[^ref-9][^ref-1]

The studio is no longer a full-time developer. In an October 2025 Patreon post, Alexander wrote that after *Order of the Thorne* "we really couldn’t go on as a full-time game development company". Its two remaining announced games, *Roehm to Ruin* and *Fortress of Fire*, are unreleased.[^ref-21]

## History

### Origins at Infamous Adventures

Before Infamous Quests, Alexander and Mills ran [[Infamous Adventures]], an amateur studio that made free remakes of Sierra adventures, among them *King's Quest III* and *Space Quest II*.[^ref-9][^ref-10][^ref-18] A 2014 Sierra Help forum post called Infamous Quests "the professional reincarnation of Infamous Adventures".[^ref-18]

### Quest for Infamy (2012–2014)

The studio announced *Quest for Infamy* in 2012. It is an adventure RPG made with Adventure Game Studio in which the player is "the morally gray William Roehm". The developers raised $63,281 for it on Kickstarter.[^ref-9] Phoenix Online Publishing released the game on 10 July 2014.[^ref-19]

### Order of the Thorne and Roehm to Ruin (2015–present)

In March 2015 Infamous Quests announced *Order of the Thorne: The King's Challenge* and a *Quest for Infamy* prequel, *Roehm to Ruin*. A Kickstarter for both succeeded on 3 May 2015, raising $30,944, and the studio then added a third game, *Order of the Thorne: Fortress of Fire*.[^ref-9] *The King's Challenge* was released on 26 January 2016.[^ref-1][^ref-22]

In the October 2025 Patreon post, Alexander wrote that the game's "financial turnout… was, to say, way under expectations". He said that "Most folks moved on to other jobs" and that for a long time he had been the main person working on the remaining games. *Roehm to Ruin* "has been completed for a while, except for some voice acting", above all sessions with the lead actor for Roehm.[^ref-21] An April 2026 post on the studio's Patreon said studio time was booked for May to finish recording Mr. Roehm's lines.[^ref-23] *Fortress of Fire* "is in a real state of limbo".[^ref-21] The vault tracks both as [[TBD - Quest for Infamy - Roehm to Ruin|Roehm to Ruin]] and [[TBD - Order of the Thorne - Fortress of Fire|Fortress of Fire]]. The studio also has a Patreon page.[^ref-5]

## Notable Games

### Quest for Infamy (2014)

*Quest for Infamy* is a point-and-click adventure RPG inspired by Sierra's Quest for Glory series. Its publisher called it "A spiritual heir to yesteryear's heroic quests".[^ref-9][^ref-19] The player chooses one of three anti-hero classes: Brigand, Rogue or Sorcerer.[^ref-17] The story begins when "A mysterious man from the north arrives into a sleepy valley", on the run from the baron of the last town he stayed in.[^ref-17]

### Order of the Thorne: The King's Challenge (2016)

In *The King's Challenge*, the player guides Finn the bard "on his quest to write ‘the greatest ballad ever’". Every ten years, King Quilhairn, ruler of the Faerie Realm, sets a challenge, and Finn takes it on.[^ref-19] Wikipedia describes the game as the first in a planned anthology series.[^ref-9]

## Key People

- **Shawn Mills**: co-founder. He is credited on *The King's Challenge* as director, writer and programmer.[^ref-9][^ref-22]
- **Steven Alexander**: co-founder. He is credited on *The King's Challenge* as producer, writer and programmer.[^ref-9][^ref-22]
- **James Broom**: listed by Wikipedia as a key person. He is credited on *The King's Challenge* as writer and animator.[^ref-9][^ref-22]
