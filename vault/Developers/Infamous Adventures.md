# Infamous Adventures

<small style="color: gray">Last updated: October 16, 2026</small>

## Overview

Infamous Adventures was an amateur adventure game studio founded in 2004 by Shawn Mills and Steven Alexander. It made free VGA remakes of classic Sierra On-Line adventure games.[^ref-13][^ref-19] Its games were built in Adventure Game Studio and given away for free. It released remakes of *King's Quest III* (2006) and *Space Quest II* (2011).[^ref-13][^ref-11] On 27 July 2012 it announced that it had stopped making games, and its founders started **[[Infamous Quests]]** to make original adventure games.[^ref-13][^ref-19][^ref-18]

Mills, who is from Australia, later wrote *The Sierra Adventure*, a history of Sierra On-Line.[^ref-10][^ref-1] Infamous Quests released *Quest for Infamy* in 2014.[^ref-13]

## History

### Founding and Early Years

Alexander's kidneys failed at the end of 2002, and he started dialysis. "About then", he and Mills "got the idea to start making video games" and founded Infamous Adventures, a studio inspired by Roberta Williams' *King's Quest* and Lori Ann and Corey Cole's *Quest for Glory*.[^ref-10] By September 2004 the team was posting on fan forums about its first projects: the Quest for Glory fan game *Quest for Infamy* and a *King's Quest III* remake.[^ref-2] Its project teams of programmers, musicians and artists came "from around the globe".[^ref-13]

The studio's first release was a remake of *King's Quest III*, which came out on 19 June 2006. It had a point-and-click interface and VGA graphics, following AGD Interactive's remakes of the first two King's Quest games.[^ref-13][^ref-21] A voice pack followed on 21 August 2006. It voiced every character and the narrator, and its cast included former Sierra staff Josh Mandel as Graham and Andy Hoyos as Manannan. The remake also added a new ending for players who finish with a perfect score.[^ref-13]

### Relationship to Sierra

Infamous Adventures never had a licence from Sierra. PC Gamer's Sierra ranking included "the AGD Interactive remakes which had an official license, but not the Infamous Adventures remakes".[^ref-3] The KQ Omnipedia says the studio later cancelled its planned Gold Editions of KQ3 and SQ2, and its own game *Kingdom of Sorrow*, "due to licensing issues and other reasons".[^ref-4]

In 2006 Adventure Gamers praised the KQ3 remake: "the quality remains as high as fans of the first two installments have come to expect". By October 2008 it had been downloaded more than 180,000 times.[^ref-21][^ref-13] In November 2006 the team released a "Project X" trailer while still working on *Quest for Infamy*.[^ref-22]

The studio confirmed a VGA remake of *Space Quest II* on 1 April 2007. It released the remake on 30 December 2011 as freeware with VGA graphics.[^ref-13][^ref-19][^ref-9]

### Closure and Transition to Infamous Quests

In a site post dated 27 July 2012, Steven Alexander and Shawn Mills announced that Infamous Adventures had stopped producing games and that they had started a new company, [[Infamous Quests]], to make original adventure games.[^ref-13][^ref-19] *Quest for Infamy* had been cancelled under Infamous Adventures. It was restarted at Infamous Quests in 2012 and released on 7 July 2014.[^ref-13]

## Notable Games

### King's Quest III Remake (2006)

[[2006 - King's Quest III Remake (Infamous Adventures)|King's Quest III Remake]] remade Roberta Williams' 1986 game with VGA graphics and a point-and-click interface. A voice pack was added in August 2006.[^ref-13][^ref-21] It is not the same game as AGD Interactive's *[[2011 - King's Quest III Redux - To Heir is Human|King's Quest III Redux]]* (2011).

### Space Quest II VGA Remake (2011)

The Space Quest II VGA remake presented the 1987 game with VGA graphics as a freeware release.[^ref-9] Released on 30 December 2011, it was the studio's final release before [[Infamous Quests]].[^ref-19][^ref-13]

## Key People

- **Steven Alexander**: co-founder.[^ref-13] He developed the studio's games while on dialysis and later received a kidney transplant. He has described both in his own words.[^ref-10]
- **Shawn Mills**: co-founder, from Australia.[^ref-13][^ref-10] He later wrote *The Sierra Adventure*.[^ref-1]
