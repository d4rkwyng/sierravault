# Infamous Adventures

<small style="color: gray">Last updated: October 26, 2026</small>

## Overview

Infamous Adventures was an amateur adventure game studio founded in 2004 by Shawn Mills and Steven Alexander. It made free VGA remakes of classic Sierra On-Line adventure games.[^ref-13][^ref-19] Its games were built in Adventure Game Studio and given away for free. It released remakes of *King's Quest III* (2006) and *Space Quest II* (2011).[^ref-13][^ref-11] On 27 July 2012 it announced that it had stopped making games, and its founders started **[[Infamous Quests]]** to make original adventure games.[^ref-13][^ref-19][^ref-18]

Mills, who is from Australia, later wrote *The Sierra Adventure*, a history of Sierra On-Line.[^ref-10][^ref-1] Infamous Quests released *Quest for Infamy* in 2014.[^ref-13]

## History

### Founding and Early Years

Alexander began dialysis at the end of 2002. "About then", he and Mills "got the idea to start making video games" and founded Infamous Adventures, a studio inspired by Roberta Williams' *King's Quest* and Lori Ann and Corey Cole's *Quest for Glory*.[^ref-10] By September 2004 the team was posting on fan forums about its first projects: the Quest for Glory fan game *Quest for Infamy* and a *King's Quest III* remake.[^ref-2] Its project teams of programmers, musicians and artists came "from around the globe".[^ref-13]

The studio's first release was a remake of *King's Quest III*, which came out on 19 June 2006. It had a point-and-click interface and VGA graphics, following AGD Interactive's remakes of the first two King's Quest games.[^ref-13][^ref-21] A voice pack followed on 20 August 2006, released alongside version 2.0 of the game.[^ref-23] It voiced every character and the narrator, and its cast included former Sierra staff Josh Mandel as Graham and Andy Hoyos as Manannan.[^ref-13][^ref-23] The 2.0 update also added a new ending for players who finish with full points.[^ref-23]

### Relationship to Sierra

Infamous Adventures never had a licence from Sierra. PC Gamer's Sierra ranking included "the AGD Interactive remakes which had an official license, but not the Infamous Adventures remakes".[^ref-3] The KQ Omnipedia says the studio later cancelled its planned Gold Editions of KQ3 and SQ2, and that its King's Quest fan game *Kingdom of Sorrow* was cancelled or put on indefinite hold "due to licensing issues and other reasons".[^ref-4]

In 2006 Adventure Gamers praised the KQ3 remake: "the quality remains as high as fans of the first two installments have come to expect". By October 2008 it had been downloaded more than 180,000 times.[^ref-21][^ref-13] By 16 November 2006 the team had posted a "Project X" trailer while still working on *Quest for Infamy*.[^ref-22] In June 2010 Project X was revealed as *Kingdom of Sorrow*.[^ref-13]

The studio confirmed a VGA remake of *Space Quest II* on 1 April 2007.[^ref-28][^ref-13] Earlier in 2011, on 15 April, the studio released *Space Quest for Glory*, a short parody of two Sierra classics.[^ref-27][^ref-11] It released the *Space Quest II* remake on 30 December 2011 as freeware with VGA graphics and a voice pack of 4,000 recorded lines.[^ref-13][^ref-19][^ref-9][^ref-29]

### Closure and Transition to Infamous Quests

In a site post dated 27 July 2012, Steven Alexander and Shawn Mills announced that Infamous Adventures had stopped producing games and that they had started a new company, [[Infamous Quests]], to make original adventure games.[^ref-13][^ref-19] *Quest for Infamy* had been cancelled under Infamous Adventures. It was restarted at Infamous Quests in 2012[^ref-13] and released on 10 July 2014.[^ref-30][^ref-31]

The Infamous Adventures name stayed in use after 2012. On 1 January 2020 it uploaded version 2.0 of its *Space Quest II* remake, with bug fixes, Sierra fonts and new Easter eggs.[^ref-24][^ref-25] A [[TBD - King's Quest IV Remake (Infamous Adventures)|King's Quest IV remake]] is attributed to it; its only recent progress report is a patron-only January 2026 update on the Patreon of Infamous Quests, the founders' separate commercial studio, which cannot be publicly read.[^ref-26]

## Notable Games

### King's Quest III Remake (2006)

[[2006 - King's Quest III Remake (Infamous Adventures)|King's Quest III Remake]] remade Roberta Williams' 1986 game with VGA graphics and a point-and-click interface. A voice pack was added in August 2006.[^ref-13][^ref-21][^ref-23] It is not the same game as AGD Interactive's *[[2011 - King's Quest III Redux - To Heir is Human|King's Quest III Redux]]* (2011).

### Space Quest II VGA Remake (2011)

The Space Quest II VGA remake presented the 1987 game with VGA graphics as a freeware release.[^ref-9] Released on 30 December 2011, it was the studio's final new game before [[Infamous Quests]]; a version 2.0 update followed in 2020.[^ref-19][^ref-13][^ref-24]
