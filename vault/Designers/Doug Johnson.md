# Doug Johnson

<small style="color: gray">Last updated: October 15, 2026</small>

## Overview

Doug Johnson is a game designer credited on Dynamix sports and flight simulations published by Sierra On-Line between 1993 and 1999.[^ref-1][^ref-2] He was lead designer on *Front Page Sports: Baseball Pro '96 Season* and *Front Page Sports: Baseball Pro '98*, one of three multiplayer designers on *Red Baron 3-D*, and the credited designer of *Curse You! Red Baron*.[^ref-25][^ref-27][^ref-28][^ref-29] Several of his credits pair design work with testing or manual writing.[^ref-1]

MobyGames lists his credits under a single profile, with no biography on file.[^ref-2][^ref-21] No interview, biography or other first-hand account of his career has been found. Other people named Doug Johnson, in education, broadcasting, software and the arts, are not the same person and are not covered here.

## Career

### Front Page Sports (1993–1997)

His earliest MobyGames credit is a "Special Thanks" listing on [[1993 - Aces Over Europe|Aces Over Europe]] (1993), Dynamix's World War II flight simulation.[^ref-30] He then worked on the Front Page Sports line. On [[1994 - Front Page Sports - Baseball Pro|Front Page Sports: Baseball '94]] he is credited with additional design and as quality assurance engineer.[^ref-22] On [[1994 - Front Page Sports - Football Pro '95|Football Pro '95]] and [[1995 - Front Page Sports - Football Pro '96|Football Pro '96 Season]] he is credited with additional design and testing.[^ref-23][^ref-24]

He was lead designer on [[1996 - Front Page Sports - Baseball Pro '96|Front Page Sports: Baseball Pro '96 Season]], which Bill Money directed.[^ref-25] He also has an additional design credit on [[1996 - Front Page Sports - Football Pro '97|Football Pro '97]].[^ref-26] On [[1997 - Front Page Sports - Baseball Pro '98|Front Page Sports: Baseball Pro '98]], directed by Glen Wolfram, he is credited with lead design, and he wrote the online and printed manuals with Michael Stearns.[^ref-27]

### Red Baron (1998–1999)

On [[1998 - Red Baron 3D|Red Baron 3-D]] (1998) he shares the multiplayer design credit with Brian Apgar and Eric Lanz. He is also credited on the manual and on the historical manual editing.[^ref-28] On [[1999 - Curse You! Red Baron|Curse You! Red Baron]] (1999) he is the credited designer. He wrote the online manual text with Dennis A. Kilgore and laid out the online manual.[^ref-29]
