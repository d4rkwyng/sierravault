# Andy Hoyos

<small style="color: gray">Last updated: October 26, 2026</small>

## Overview

Andy Hoyos is an American illustrator, painter and art director who worked at Sierra On-Line from about 1989–1990 to 1996.[^ref-3][^ref-13] According to MobyGames' biography, he was hired as a 2D computer animator and went on to art direct many games, including EcoQuest 2, King's Quest V and VII, Quest for Glory III and the Phantasmagoria series; in the game credits themselves he is Art Designer on King's Quest V and Director on Phantasmagoria 2.[^ref-13] MobyGames credits him on 23 games.[^ref-2] VGA Gallery credits him with "spearheading the look" of games such as King's Quest V, Quest for Glory III and Phantasmagoria.[^ref-6]

Before entering the game industry, Hoyos worked as a graphic artist and illustrator for NBC television studios in Burbank, California, and then in art and design for commercial animation studios and film advertising.[^ref-3] He directed the live-action sequences of Phantasmagoria: A Puzzle of Flesh (1996).[^ref-4]

## Career

### Early Career

Born in Los Angeles, Hoyos attended Art Center College of Design in Pasadena.[^ref-14] He dates the start of his art career to 1972, as a graphic artist and illustrator at NBC in Burbank;[^ref-3] the MobyGames biography gives 1971 and says the NBC job lasted until 1980, with NBC putting him through Art Center.[^ref-13] He then worked as a freelance illustrator and advertising designer.[^ref-13]

From 1986 to 1989 he turned to animation: according to MobyGames he was a designer and background painter for Kurtz and Friends and Duck Soup Productions, worked at Marvel Productions, and at Calico Productions worked on *Denver, the Last Dinosaur* and *The Chipmunks*.[^ref-13] Wikipedia lists World Events Productions and Calico Entertainment among Denver's production companies.[^ref-15]

### Sierra Years (1989–1996)

Hoyos writes that in 1989 he "accepted an art directorship" with Sierra On-Line;[^ref-3] MobyGames dates his Sierra years 1990–1996 and says he was hired as a 2D computer animator before he began art directing.[^ref-13]

His early Sierra credits include assistant art director on the 1990 [[1990 - Oils Well|Oil's Well]] and animator/illustrator on [[1990 - Conquests of Camelot - The Search for the Grail|Conquests of Camelot: The Search for the Grail]] (1990).[^ref-13][^ref-11] On [[1990 - King's Quest V - Absence Makes the Heart Go Yonder|King's Quest V]] (1990) he is credited as Art Designer, and he also voiced the wizard Mordack.[^ref-13] In 2014 VGA Gallery released a print of his King's Quest V background "Wizard's Island", showing Mordack's island castle.[^ref-6]

In 1991 he was a background artist on [[1991 - Space Quest I - Roger Wilco in the Sarien Encounter|Space Quest I VGA]], [[1991 - Leisure Suit Larry 5 - Passionate Patti Does a Little Undercover Work|Leisure Suit Larry 5]] and [[1991 - Mixed-Up Fairy Tales|Mixed-Up Fairy Tales]], an artist on [[1990 - Jones in the Fast Lane|Jones in the Fast Lane]], and art designer and production designer on [[1991 - Castle of Dr. Brain|Castle of Dr. Brain]].[^ref-13][^ref-12] On Leisure Suit Larry 5 he is credited for graphics and production; the game's art directors were Jane Cardinal and Cheryl Loyd.[^ref-16]

He was production designer on [[1992 - The Dagger of Amon Ra|The Dagger of Amon Ra]] (1992), sketching designs for its characters,[^ref-13][^ref-8] and art director on [[1992 - Quest for Glory III - Wages of War|Quest for Glory III: Wages of War]] (1992), [[1993 - EcoQuest - Lost Secret of the Rainforest|EcoQuest 2: Lost Secret of the Rainforest]] (1993) and [[1994 - King's Quest VII - The Princeless Bride|King's Quest VII: The Princeless Bride]] (1994), where he is also credited as one of the directors.[^ref-13]

On the original [[1995 - Phantasmagoria|Phantasmagoria]] (1995) he was art director and is credited as a game director and writer.[^ref-13][^ref-7] Wikipedia says he took part in brainstorming with Roberta Williams during the writing stage and conceived most of the death scenes; he designed the rooms, furniture, textures and lighting in Alias, and the rendered rooms became the game's backgrounds.[^ref-7] The Dot Eaters describes Williams spending six months preparing for the game, "studying the genre closely along with the art director for the project, Andy Hoyos."[^ref-8] On [[1996 - Phantasmagoria - A Puzzle of Flesh|Phantasmagoria 2: A Puzzle of Flesh]] (1996), Hoyos directed the live-action sequences.[^ref-4]

### Post-Sierra Career

After Sierra, Hoyos was "Art Director and 3D artist for The Matrix Online",[^ref-13] Monolith Productions' 2005 MMORPG.[^ref-10] MobyGames also credits him with art management on Ultima IX: Ascension (1999), concept art on Shadowrun (2007), and art on several Jewel Quest games (2009–2011),[^ref-13] a series created and published by iWin.[^ref-21] His 2016 website biography names Origin Systems among the companies he worked with and says he art directed Ultima Online 2; it adds environment concept work on Shadowrun and Forza 4, and says he retired from games in 2013.[^ref-14]

He later returned for one more adventure, sharing the art direction credit (with Michael Fortunato) on [[2014 - Moebius - Empire Rising|Moebius: Empire Rising]] (2014), Jane Jensen's game developed by Pinkerton Road and Phoenix Online Studios.[^ref-13][^ref-17][^ref-18] He also voiced the wizard Manannan in Infamous Adventures' 2006 VGA remake of King's Quest III; MobyGames' description of the remake calls him "the man who provided the voice of Mordack in King's Quest V, bringing life to Mordack's wicked brother Manannan."[^ref-20] (MobyGames' biography of Hoyos also lists Mordack among his roles in the remake, which no other source found confirms.)[^ref-13] His performance shipped in the voice pack released with version 2.0 on August 20, 2006, after the game's June 19, 2006 release; the studio's announcement says "about half of the Manannan files" he had recorded were lost and some parts had to be re-recorded.[^ref-19][^ref-20]

His current work is digital, printed on etching paper; he describes exploring "Abstraction."[^ref-3]

## Notable Works

### King's Quest V: Absence Makes the Heart Go Yonder (1990)

Hoyos is credited as Art Designer on [[1990 - King's Quest V - Absence Makes the Heart Go Yonder|King's Quest V]] and as the voice of Mordack.[^ref-13] VGA Gallery's 2014 print "Wizard's Island" reproduces his background of Mordack's island castle, which it calls one of "the most signature moments" of the game.[^ref-6]

### Conquests of Camelot: The Search for the Grail (1990)

On [[1990 - Conquests of Camelot - The Search for the Grail|Conquests of Camelot]], the game credits list Hoyos among the animators with Douglas Herring, Peter Ledger, Kenn Nishiuye and Jennifer Shontz, and a later version credits him among the illustrators.[^ref-11]

### Space Quest I VGA (1991)

Hoyos was one of nine background artists on [[1991 - Space Quest I - Roger Wilco in the Sarien Encounter|Space Quest I VGA]], alongside Jay Allan Friedmann, Douglas Herring and others.[^ref-12]

### Quest for Glory III: Wages of War (1992)

Hoyos was art director on [[1992 - Quest for Glory III - Wages of War|Quest for Glory III]].[^ref-13] VGA Gallery names it among the games whose look he spearheaded.[^ref-6]

### Phantasmagoria (1995)

As art director on [[1995 - Phantasmagoria|Phantasmagoria]], Hoyos drew inspiration from Tim Burton's films, especially Batman (1989), and also from Alien and Hellraiser, according to Wikipedia.[^ref-7]

### Phantasmagoria 2: A Puzzle of Flesh (1996)

[[1996 - Phantasmagoria - A Puzzle of Flesh|Phantasmagoria 2]]'s live-action sequences were written by Lorelei Shannon, directed by Hoyos, filmed by cinematographer Matthew Jensen and edited by Wes Plate.[^ref-4] MobyGames also credits him with creature design.[^ref-13]

### The Matrix Online (2005)

After leaving Sierra, Hoyos served as Art Director and 3D artist for *The Matrix Online*,[^ref-13] the MMORPG developed by Monolith Productions.[^ref-10]

## Legacy

VGA Gallery credits Hoyos with spearheading the look of King's Quest V, Quest for Glory III and Phantasmagoria, and says he "helped elevate Sierra to one of the most successful game companies of the time".[^ref-6]
