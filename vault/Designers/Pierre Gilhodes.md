# Pierre Gilhodes

<small style="color: gray">Last updated: November 12, 2026</small>

## Overview

Pierre Gilhodes is a French artist and game designer best known for his work on the Gobliiins series.[^ref-6] He presented his work to Coktel Vision in 1989, co-created Gobliiins with [[Muriel Tramis]], and since 2009 has continued the series himself, most recently as an independent developer with [[2023 - Gobliiins 5 - The Morgloton Invasion|Gobliiins 5]] (2023) and [[2026 - Gobliins 6|Gobliins 6]] (2026).[^ref-6][^ref-21][^ref-26]

Gilhodes studied desktop publishing (DTP) and drawing.[^ref-6] Of his recent games he has said: "I am not a programmer and I use a tool that I cannot completely parameterize."[^ref-4]

## Career

### Early Career

Gilhodes studied DTP and drawing and presented his work to Coktel Vision in 1989.[^ref-6] A biography on his former personal website instead says he began creating video games with Coktel Vision in 1990; the two sources differ on the start year, and his earliest credit, Asterix: Operation Getafix, is dated 1989.[^ref-35][^ref-21] His earliest credited work was Asterix: Operation Getafix (1989), where he is credited for graphics as "Pierre," followed by graphics for Geisha and Los Tres Cerditos Se Divierten in 1990.[^ref-21]

### Coktel Vision Years

MobyGames credits the original concept of [[1991 - Gobliiins|Gobliiins]] (1991) to Gilhodes and Muriel Tramis at Coktel Vision.[^ref-30] Tramis herself has said that the game's system of cooperating characters "belongs primarily to Pierre Gilhodes".[^ref-29] Coktel was an independent French studio at the time; Sierra On-Line acquired the company on 29 October 1993.[^ref-39][^ref-27] Gilhodes went on to [[1992 - Gobliins 2 - The Prince Buffoon|Gobliins 2: The Prince Buffoon]] (1992) and [[1993 - Goblins Quest 3|Goblins Quest 3]] (1993), both credited "Created by".[^ref-28] IMDb lists him under graphics on both.[^ref-8] The French magazine Tilt introduced him in 1993 as the graphic artist and co-writer, with Muriel Tramis, of Goblins 3.[^ref-3]

In the same period he illustrated Once Upon a Time: Abracadabra and Once Upon a Time: Baba Yaga (1991).[^ref-28] The Sierra release of [[1992 - Inca|Inca]] (1992) credits it as "Created by Pierre Gilhodes", with Muriel Tramis managing the project; sources disagree on who should be credited as its designer (see the game page).[^ref-7][^ref-28]

MobyGames' biography calls [[1995 - The Bizarre Adventures of Woodruff and the Schnibble|The Bizarre Adventures of Woodruff and the Schnibble]] (1995) the last game he worked on for Coktel; it carried over the visual style of Gobliiins, and MobyGames credits him with its scenario and graphics.[^ref-6][^ref-21] His MobyGames credits also list graphics on [[1995 - Playtoons 4 - The Mandarine Prince|Playtoons 4]] and [[1995 - Playtoons 5 - The Stone of Wakan|Playtoons 5]] (both 1995), and a "The following also participated" credit on [[1996 - Urban Runner|Urban Runner]] (1996).[^ref-21][^ref-28] In a 2017 retrospective, jeuxvideo.com called Woodruff "un ovni dans le monde vidéoludique" that marked players with its "côté atypique et décalé", and interviewed Gilhodes as "créateur du jeu".[^ref-2]

### Later Career

In the 2000s Gilhodes worked as an artist and animator for other studios: 3D animation on Ben Hur: Blood of Braves (2003) for MAS Productions and 2D/3D art on Bet on Soldier: Blood Sport (2005) for Kylotonn.[^ref-21]

#### Wizarbox Period (2008–2012)

At the French studio Wizarbox he contributed character animation and 3D art to So Blonde (2008), Scrabble Interactive: 2009 Edition (2009), Gray Matter (2010, additional artist), So Blonde: Back to the Island (2010), Captain Morgane and the Golden Turtle (2012) and R.A.W: Realms of Ancient War (2012).[^ref-21][^ref-22][^ref-23][^ref-24]

#### Return to Gobliiins

Disappointed by the lack of effort to produce another Gobliiins game, Gilhodes assembled a team called Société Pollene and designed [[2009 - Gobliiins 4|Gobliiins 4]] (2009).[^ref-6] He crowdfunded [[2023 - Gobliiins 5 - The Morgloton Invasion|Gobliiins 5: The Morgloton Invasion]] on Ulule, where the campaign ran from February 19 to March 21, 2022 and raised €6,950 against a €3,000 goal.[^ref-33] He released the game in 2023 as an independent developer, credited for its scenario, programming, graphics and animations.[^ref-21]

On itch.io he announced its successor: "Le suivant est commencé, Gobliins6, qui est la suite de Gobliins2. Il y aura un crowdfunding dans quelques temps."[^ref-4] [[2026 - Gobliins 6|Gobliins 6]] was crowdfunded on two platforms. The Ulule campaign ran from October 9 to November 13, 2024 and raised €5,040 against a €4,000 goal.[^ref-32] The Kickstarter campaign ran from October 10 to November 9, 2024, also against a €4,000 goal; its page now shows €11,448 pledged by 470 backers.[^ref-25] According to a Planète Aventure forum post relaying his August 2025 Kickstarter update, Muriel Tramis helped write the scenario of two of its levels, the 2nd and the 12th.[^ref-38] The game was released on itch.io on February 10, 2026, and on Steam on February 13, 2026, where the publisher is [[Schnibble Productions]].[^ref-26][^ref-37]

#### Comics

Gilhodes also keeps an illustration blog, Le Blog du Bouzouk, which MobyGames links from his profile; a post there titled "Bouzouk - MegaDreamPark" was published under his name on 4 May 2011.[^ref-6][^ref-34] When a player on itch.io asked whether the comic "Bouzouk MegaDream Park" would be translated into English, he replied: "The comic Bouzouk is well translated into English."[^ref-16] In a later itch.io reply he said that in a month or two he would launch a crowdfunding campaign "pour obtenir certains livres en vraies éditions papier, dont Bouzouk MegaDreamPark" (to get certain books into real paper editions, including Bouzouk MegaDreamPark).[^ref-4]

## Notable Works

### Gobliiins (1991)

Gilhodes told Tilt in 1993 that the game began as something else: "Dans sa toute première mouture, Goblins 1 (Gobliiins) était à l'origine un jeu d'action mettant en scène trois personnages bougeant simultanément et contrôlés indépendamment. Mais des contraintes de programmation m'ont forcé à le transformer en jeu d'aventure, tout en gardant le principe des trois personnages."[^ref-3] Muriel Tramis credits the game's system of three characters with complementary talents primarily to Gilhodes.[^ref-29]

### Goblins Quest 3 (1993)

For [[1993 - Goblins Quest 3|Goblins Quest 3]], Gilhodes and Tramis reworked the puzzles with testers to make them more accessible, and the code was written by the MDO team.[^ref-3] The game's shadow-control idea had a cinematic source: "L'idée du contrôle de l'ombre m'est venue après avoir vu le film Dracula."[^ref-3]

### The Bizarre Adventures of Woodruff and the Schnibble (1995)

MobyGames' biography calls [[1995 - The Bizarre Adventures of Woodruff and the Schnibble|Woodruff]] the last game Gilhodes worked on for Coktel, though his credits also include graphics on Playtoons 4 and 5 (1995).[^ref-6][^ref-21] Asked about a sequel, he replied on itch.io: "Woodruff's sequel is a complex project, especially for a single person... It's not for now. I'm starting Gobliins6 for now."[^ref-16]

### Gobliiins 5: The Morgloton Invasion (2023)

MobyGames credits Gilhodes with Gobliiins 5's scenario, programming, graphics and animations.[^ref-21] The game was released on itch.io on 2 May 2023 and on Steam on 20 July 2023.[^ref-41][^ref-40] When a player suggested switching the game's scaling method to linear interpolation for a smoother picture, he replied on itch.io: "It's true, it's smoother like that, but less pixel art".[^ref-16]

## Design Philosophy

Gilhodes works within the limits of his tools rather than as a programmer. Answering player feedback on Gobliiins 5, he wrote: "I would like to consider your message for the next game but it is not always possible and it is already well started. I am not a programmer and I use a tool that I cannot completely parameterize."[^ref-4][^ref-16]

As a player, he told Tilt in 1993: "Ma préférence va aux jeux d'action et d'aventure, avec une passion pour Tetris."[^ref-3]

## Legacy

Gilhodes has continued the Gobliiins series for more than three decades, from the Coktel games of 1991–1993 to Gobliiins 4 (2009), Gobliiins 5 (2023) and the crowdfunded Gobliins 6 (2026).[^ref-21][^ref-25][^ref-26] In 2025 he was interviewed about his career on the New Game Old Flame podcast.[^ref-13] In an earlier itch.io reply he wrote that "The Switch and other consoles version is well planned,"[^ref-16] and he later confirmed to a player: "Oui, la compil est dispo sur Switch."[^ref-16] On 27 May 2026 Red Art Games (with developer Red Art Studios) released the Gobliiins Collection on Switch, PS5 and Xbox Series: five games, Gobliiins through Gobliiins 5, without Gobliins 6. Its bonus content includes a mini-series and gallery presenting Gilhodes and an interview with him about the origins of the series, and a PS5 physical edition was announced to follow in September.[^ref-36]
