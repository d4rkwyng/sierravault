# Rabih AbouJaoudé

<small style="color: gray">Last updated: October 26, 2026</small>

## Overview

Rabih AbouJaoudé (also credited as Rabih Aboujaoude) is an artist and game designer whose credits run from Sierra's Hoyle casual-game line in the mid-1990s to RealNetworks' GameHouse Studios at the end of the 2000s.[^ref-1][^ref-15] As of October 26, 2026, MobyGames lists 66 credits on 34 games for him, from Hoyle Classic Games (1995) to The Dream Voyagers (2010), plus a 2011 "special thanks" credit on the Windows Phone version of Collapse!.[^ref-16] His roles include Designer, Art Director, Lead Artist and 3D Illustrator.[^ref-16]

Almost all of his Sierra-era credits are on Hoyle games. MobyGames' Hoyle licensees group, which "includes both the 'old' Sierra and 'new' Sierra Hoyle games", lists 52 titles.[^ref-2] By mid-2008 he was credited on GameHouse titles, including entries in the Little Shop hidden-object series.[^ref-16][^ref-17]

## Career

### Sierra (1995–2005)

Sierra On-Line's employee list at The Sierra Chest includes him ("Aboujaoude Rabih").[^ref-15] His earliest MobyGames credit is as Lead Artist on Hoyle Classic Games (1995).[^ref-5][^ref-16] In 1996 he was Lead Artist on Hoyle Bridge and Hoyle Children's Collection and one of the Character Animators on Hoyle Blackjack.[^ref-16] Sierra's Holiday 1996 InterAction profile of the Hoyle team calls him lead artist in its account of how the [[1996 - Hoyle Casino|Hoyle Casino]] characters were made. Producer Steve Van Horn said: "Rabih AbouJaoudé, lead artist, and fellow artist Heather Ivy would draw renditions of what sort of characters should be found around the Blackjack and Poker tables."[^ref-18] MobyGames does not list a Hoyle Casino (1996) credit for him.[^ref-16]

In the late 1990s his credits widened from art into design. He is credited under Design as well as Graphics/Artwork on Hoyle Board Games (1998) and Hoyle Card Games (1999), and among the Designers of Hoyle Backgammon & Cribbage (1999).[^ref-6][^ref-16]

In the 2000s he took on design and art direction roles. He was a Designer and Lead Artist on Hoyle Kids Games (2000)[^ref-8], Lead Artist on the Macintosh and Dreamcast releases of Hoyle Casino (2000), where he is also among the Designers of the Macintosh version,[^ref-16] Art Director on Hoyle Casino (2001)[^ref-7], Designer and Art Director on Hoyle Table Games 2004 (2003), a Sierra release developed by Buzz Monkey Software,[^ref-19][^ref-16] and did additional design and art direction on the 2005 Card Games, Board Games and Puzzle Games editions.[^ref-16] On Hoyle Poker Series (2005), which MobyGames lists as developed by Sierra Entertainment and published by Encore Software, he is credited among the Designers and as Lead Artist - 3D Illustrator.[^ref-14][^ref-16]

### Hoyle Puzzle & Board Games (2009, Encore)

His last Hoyle credit is on Hoyle Puzzle & Board Games (2009, Windows and Macintosh; also titled Hoyle Puzzle & Board Games 2010), where MobyGames lists him among the Designers and as Art Director.[^ref-9][^ref-16] This was not a Sierra release: MobyGames lists Encore Software, Inc. as publisher, and Encore Software, Inc. and Freeverse, Inc. as developers.[^ref-9]

### GameHouse (2008–2010)

His first GameHouse credits date from 2008. GameHouse Studios was "Real's Seattle-based" casual games studio, and Matt Turetzky, a vice president in Real's Games Division, called Little Shop "the top-selling franchise across our portals and distribution network".[^ref-3] The Little Shop games are hidden-object titles developed by GameHouse.[^ref-17]

He is credited with Graphic Design on Ancient Secrets: Quest for the Golden Key (2008)[^ref-11], Little Shop: Road Trip (2008), Little Shop: Memories (2009), Little Shop: World Traveler (2009)[^ref-10] and Sunset Studio: Love on the High Seas (2009).[^ref-16] He is listed among the Artists on Collapse! (2009)[^ref-12] and Marooned (2009).[^ref-16] His last game credit is under Art on The Dream Voyagers (2010), as "Zee"; the game's credits give team members dream-themed nicknames, such as "Sandman" and "Morpheus".[^ref-13]

### Later

His LinkedIn profile (as of October 2026) describes a "10+ year career in casual games", lists Seward Park Clay Studio, and gives his location as Seattle.[^ref-4]

## Notable Works

### Hoyle Puzzle & Board Games (2009)

He is credited among the Designers and as Art Director on this Encore Software release for Windows and Macintosh.[^ref-9][^ref-16]

### Hoyle Poker Series (2005)

On this poker collection he is credited among the Designers and as Lead Artist - 3D Illustrator.[^ref-14][^ref-16]

### Hoyle Card Games (1999)

He is credited under Design and Graphics/Artwork.[^ref-6][^ref-16]

### Little Shop Series (2008–2009)

At GameHouse he did graphic design on three Little Shop games: Road Trip, Memories and World Traveler.[^ref-10][^ref-16][^ref-17]
