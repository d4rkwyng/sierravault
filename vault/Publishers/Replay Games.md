# Replay Games

<small style="color: gray">Last updated: November 11, 2026</small>

## Overview

Replay Games was an American game publisher based in Austin, Texas, founded by Paul Trowe in 2008.[^ref-16][^ref-17] GamesBeat reported that Trowe "bootstrapped his Austin, Texas-based company in 2008 to focus on the digital distribution of games".[^ref-17] Its link to Sierra came through the *Leisure Suit Larry* series, licensed from intellectual property holder Codemasters: Replay announced in October 2011 that it had secured the rights (GamesBeat dates the deal to 2010) and began remaking the games in high definition with creator [[Al Lowe]].[^ref-19][^ref-22][^ref-23][^ref-6]

## Sierra Connection

In October 2011 Replay announced that it had secured the rights to the *Leisure Suit Larry* franchise and would release HD versions of past titles, starting with the 1987 original, working with Al Lowe.[^ref-19] Trowe said: "I've been working to acquire the original Sierra properties for over 10 years."[^ref-19] GamesBeat notes that Vivendi had sold the Larry franchise to Codemasters, which published *Leisure Suit Larry: Box Office Bust* in 2009; Wikipedia describes Codemasters as the intellectual property holder that Replay worked with.[^ref-17][^ref-6]

Replay funded the first remake with a single Kickstarter campaign in April–May 2012, which raised $655,182 from 14,081 backers against a $500,000 goal.[^ref-1] GamesBeat reported a total of $673,602 when the campaign closed, citing additional PayPal donations.[^ref-22] *Leisure Suit Larry: Reloaded*, developed by N-Fusion Interactive, was released on 27 June 2013.[^ref-4][^ref-6] Lowe had been recruited to work on HD remakes of six of the first seven Larry games, but on 11 December 2013 it was announced that he had left Replay and returned to retirement.[^ref-8] Replay's statement framed the exit as amicable; Lowe told VentureBeat that his agreement with Replay "was for a two-year period, which recently ended" and that he had decided not to renew it.[^ref-30]

## Games Published (Sierra Archive)

- **2013** — [[2013 - Fester Mudd - Curse of the Gold|Fester Mudd: Curse of the Gold]] — Notes: Original adventure game, developed by Prank Ltd.[^ref-15][^ref-18]

## Key People

- **Paul Trowe**: Founder and CEO. GamesBeat reports that "Trowe was the first teenage beta tester at Sierra On-Line".[^ref-17][^ref-19]
- **[[Josh Mandel]]**: Chief Creative Officer, hired in December 2012. He was formerly director of Sierra On-Line's design department, and worked as a designer and writer on *Leisure Suit Larry: Reloaded* (Wikipedia lists him as a consultant on *Fester Mudd*) before leaving some time after Reloaded's release.[^ref-18][^ref-24]
- **[[Al Lowe]]**: Leisure Suit Larry creator; worked with Replay on the remakes until December 2013.[^ref-19][^ref-8]

## Later Years

No source found gives a closing date for Replay Games. *Leisure Suit Larry: Reloaded* was delisted from Steam on 24 November 2017, most likely because the licence from Codemasters had expired; it remained on sale at GOG.com.[^ref-20][^ref-6] Steam's app page for the game now says it "is no longer available on the Steam store", while GOG still lists it at $9.95 (both checked 11 November 2026).[^ref-25][^ref-3] By January 2019 the replaygamesinc.com domain showed an unrelated mobile-game publishing site aimed at the Arabic-speaking market.[^ref-21] Replay's own store was still advertising *Reloaded* as "NOW AVAILABLE" in December 2014.[^ref-29] Archived captures of the domain from May 2021 and January 2024 show only a "We'll Be Back Soon" holding page.[^ref-27][^ref-26] The live domain no longer loads a site.
