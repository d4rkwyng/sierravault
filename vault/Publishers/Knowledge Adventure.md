# Knowledge Adventure

<small style="color: gray">Last updated: October 26, 2026</small>

> [!note] Full history
> This page covers Knowledge Adventure as a publisher of Dr. Brain games. The company's full, sourced history is on the developer page: [[Developers/Knowledge Adventure|Knowledge Adventure (developer)]].

## Overview

Knowledge Adventure was an American educational software developer and publisher, founded in California in 1991 by Rick Gibson and the brothers Bill and Larry Gross. It is best known for the *JumpStart* series.[^ref-1][^ref-2] CUC International disclosed its plan to buy the company in November 1996[^ref-26] and completed the purchase on 31 January 1997, making it part of the CUC Software division alongside Sierra On-Line.[^ref-1] From then on Knowledge Adventure was a **sister company** to Sierra, not a Sierra studio.[^ref-17][^ref-3] When CUC became Cendant in December 1997, the group became Cendant Software.[^ref-3] Cendant sold that division to Havas, already a subsidiary of Vivendi; the sale closed on 12 January 1999, and the group became Havas Interactive.[^ref-25][^ref-1] In October 2004 Vivendi Universal Games **sold** Knowledge Adventure to the venture capital firms Azure Capital Partners and Telesoft Partners, and it became part of Knowledge Holdings Inc.[^ref-16][^ref-2] The company renamed itself JumpStart Games in October 2012, was acquired by NetDragon Websoft in July 2017, and closed on 1 July 2023.[^ref-1][^ref-27][^ref-28]

## Sierra Connection

The *Dr. Brain* series was created at Sierra by [[Corey Cole]], who designed the first game, *Castle of Dr. Brain* (1991).[^ref-19] After the 1997 CUC purchase, Knowledge Adventure's games group made new *Dr. Brain* titles. Jim Winstead Jr., who worked in that group, wrote that "KA and Sierra had wound up under the same corporate umbrella".[^ref-18] Knowledge Adventure's *Dr. Brain* games came out in 1998 and 1999, followed by a 2011 *JumpStart* title.[^ref-20][^ref-21][^ref-22][^ref-23]
