# Encore Software

<small style="color: gray">Last updated: October 16, 2026</small>

## Overview

Encore Software, LLC is an American software publisher; its own site lists personal productivity, utility, education and gaming titles.[^ref-1][^ref-2] It was founded in October 1994 by Michael Bell and Michael Wegmann in a basement apartment in Venice, California.[^ref-4][^ref-2]

Encore matters to the Sierra archive because it continued the Hoyle series. In December 2004 VU Games assigned the Hoyle games licence to Encore, which went on to publish Hoyle card, casino, and puzzle and board game titles.[^ref-15][^ref-16]

## History

### Founding and Early Growth (1994-2001)

Encore's early business came from bundling previously released software.[^ref-4][^ref-2] It then began developing original content and entered the educational market, in 1998 according to MobyGames and in 2000 according to Encore's own history page,[^ref-4][^ref-2] with titles such as the *Math Advantage* series.[^ref-2][^ref-8] MobyGames says the company grew an average of 80% per year during its first six years,[^ref-4][^ref-3] and Encore says it made Inc. Magazine's list of the fastest-growing privately held companies.[^ref-2]

### Navarre Era (2002-2014)

In 2002 Encore became a majority-owned subsidiary of Navarre Corporation, a Minneapolis-based distributor of home entertainment and multimedia software. MobyGames and Encore date this to August 2002, while Navarre's own annual report says "we acquired Encore" in July 2002.[^ref-4][^ref-2][^ref-19] On March 31, 2003, Encore signed a publishing agreement with JoWooD Productions Software AG for the marketing and distribution of nine games throughout North America.[^ref-4]

On April 13, 2004, Navarre announced that Encore had reached "an exclusive co-publishing agreement for the sales and marketing of Riverdeep products in the United States for a majority of the retail marketplace", with full management to begin in early 2005.[^ref-18] Riverdeep owned [[Broderbund Software|Broderbund]] at the time.

Later that year VU Games announced an agreement with Encore that included "the assignment of the Hoyle games license and a worldwide perpetual license for certain family entertainment software content and technology". From December 23, 2004, Encore handled sales, distribution and marketing for the Hoyle products, while VU Games kept certain OEM, foreign, digital distribution and online gaming rights.[^ref-15][^ref-16]

### Broderbund License

On November 11, 2008 (the year is per Wikipedia; the archived release shows only "Nov. 11"), Navarre announced an expanded agreement giving Encore "the exclusive publishing rights in North American for the Broderbund(R) line of products in the retail, direct-to-consumer and OEM channels". Encore also took over online sales at Broderbund.com; before this it had exclusively distributed those titles in US and Canadian retail.[^ref-17][^ref-1]

## Sierra Connection

The Hoyle series began in 1989 and was initially developed and published by Sierra On-Line.[^ref-21] VU Games handed the Hoyle licence to Encore at the end of 2004.[^ref-15] Encore released Hoyle-branded card, casino, and puzzle and board game titles from 2005; the last Encore Hoyle title MobyGames lists is Hoyle Card Games 2012 (2011).[^ref-4]

## Current Status

Encore Software continues to operate as a software publisher. Besides retail publishing, it sells directly to customers through its managed sites.[^ref-2] Its contact page gives an address in Novato, California.[^ref-10]
