# Encore Software

<small style="color: gray">Last updated: October 26, 2026</small>

## Overview

Encore Software, LLC is an American software publisher; its own site lists personal productivity, utility, education and gaming titles.[^ref-1][^ref-2] It was founded in October 1994 by Michael Bell and Michael Wegmann in a basement apartment in Venice, California.[^ref-4][^ref-2]

Encore matters to the Sierra archive because it continued the Hoyle series. In December 2004 Encore signed an exclusive five-year licence for the Hoyle brand with The United States Playing Card Company, and VU Games assigned it the Hoyle games licence; Encore went on to publish Hoyle card, casino, and puzzle and board game titles.[^ref-22][^ref-15][^ref-16]

## History

### Founding and Early Growth (1994-2001)

Encore's early business came from bundling previously released software.[^ref-4][^ref-2] It then began developing original content and entered the educational market, in 1998 according to MobyGames and in 2000 according to Encore's own history page,[^ref-4][^ref-2] with titles such as the *Math Advantage* series.[^ref-2][^ref-8] MobyGames says the company grew an average of 80% per year during its first six years,[^ref-4][^ref-3] and Encore says it made Inc. Magazine's list of the fastest-growing privately held companies.[^ref-2]

### Navarre Era (2002-2014)

In 2002 Encore became a majority-owned subsidiary of Navarre Corporation, a Minneapolis-based distributor of home entertainment and multimedia software. On August 5, 2002, Navarre announced that it had acquired "the primary assets of Encore Software, Inc." from the United States Bankruptcy Court for the Central District of California, with founder Mike Bell staying on to run Encore day to day.[^ref-24] MobyGames and Encore date this to August 2002, while Navarre's own annual report says "we acquired Encore" in July 2002.[^ref-4][^ref-2][^ref-19] On March 31, 2003, Encore signed a publishing agreement with JoWooD Productions Software AG for the marketing and distribution of nine games throughout North America.[^ref-4]

On April 13, 2004, Navarre announced that Encore had reached "an exclusive co-publishing agreement for the sales and marketing of Riverdeep products in the United States for a majority of the retail marketplace", with full management to begin in early 2005.[^ref-18] Riverdeep owned [[Broderbund Software|Broderbund]] at the time.[^ref-18]

On December 21, 2004, Navarre announced that Encore had reached an exclusive licensing agreement with The United States Playing Card Company giving it "the worldwide publishing rights to the Hoyle brand for PC within the majority of the retail and electronic download channels" for five years.[^ref-22] Navarre's 2009 credit agreement dates this "Licensing and Distribution Agreement" between Encore and the card company to December 17, 2004, and still listed it among Encore's licences in November 2009.[^ref-23] The same month VU Games announced an agreement with Encore that included "the assignment of the Hoyle games license and a worldwide perpetual license for certain family entertainment software content and technology". From December 23, 2004, Encore handled sales, distribution and marketing for the Hoyle products, while VU Games kept certain OEM, foreign, digital distribution and online gaming rights.[^ref-15][^ref-16]

### Broderbund License

On November 11, 2008, Navarre announced an expanded agreement giving Encore "the exclusive publishing rights in North American for the Broderbund(R) line of products in the retail, direct-to-consumer and OEM channels". Encore also took over online sales at Broderbund.com; before this it had exclusively distributed those titles in US and Canadian retail.[^ref-17][^ref-1] The archived release shows only "Nov. 11"; the year comes from Wikipedia and fits the two Encore–Riverdeep licence agreements (a "Channel License Agreement" and a manufacturing-rights licence) that Navarre's 2009 credit agreement dates to July 1, 2008.[^ref-1][^ref-23]

### After Navarre (2014-2017)

On July 9, 2014, Speed Commerce, Inc., as Navarre Corporation had been renamed, sold substantially all of the assets of its retail distribution and software publishing business (the "Navarre Business Assets") to Wynit Distribution, LLC.[^ref-25][^ref-26] By 2017 the business was held as WD Encore Software, LLC, one of the Wynit companies that filed for Chapter 11 bankruptcy in Minnesota on September 8, 2017. On November 9, 2017, the court approved the sale of Encore's business and assets to Sereno Ventures, LLC for $1.7 million; the sale closed on November 13, 2017.[^ref-27]

## Sierra Connection

The Hoyle series began in 1989 and was initially developed and published by Sierra On-Line.[^ref-21] VU Games handed the Hoyle licence to Encore at the end of 2004.[^ref-15] Encore released Hoyle-branded card, casino, and puzzle and board game titles from 2005 through 2016.[^ref-21]

## Current Status

Encore Software, LLC continues to operate as a software publisher. Besides retail publishing, it sells directly to customers through its managed sites.[^ref-2] Its contact page gives an address in Novato, California.[^ref-10]
