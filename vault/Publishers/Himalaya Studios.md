# Himalaya Studios

<small style="color: gray">Last updated: October 16, 2026</small>

## Overview

Himalaya Studios is an independent adventure game studio. It was established in 2004 in Chandler, Arizona, by Britney K. Brimhall and Christopher T. Warren, and was incorporated as a C corporation in Delaware in 2011.[^ref-2][^ref-1] Brimhall and Warren had earlier co-founded [[Publishers/AGD Interactive|AGD Interactive]], known for its free Sierra remakes; Prima Games calls Himalaya "the commercial side of AGD Interactive".[^ref-1][^ref-9]

For detailed information about the studio's history, notable games, and key people, see [[Developers/Himalaya Studios|Himalaya Studios (Developer)]].

## Publishing History

Himalaya publishes its own games digitally. Its debut, *Al Emmo and the Lost Dutchman's Mine*, came out in 2006; some later regional and retail editions were published by third parties (rondomedia in Germany in 2007, Meridian4 in 2008, United Independent Entertainment in Germany in 2012).[^ref-7][^ref-5] *Mage's Initiation: Reign of the Elements* was funded on Kickstarter in 2013 (3,157 backers pledged $125,174) and released on 30 January 2019. Himalaya Studios developed and published it.[^ref-8][^ref-3][^ref-16] Adventure Gamers called it "a game more than a full decade in the making".[^ref-6]
