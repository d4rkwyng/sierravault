# AGD Interactive

<small style="color: gray">Last updated: October 26, 2026</small>

## Overview

AGD Interactive (AGDI, "Anonymous Game Developers Interactive") makes enhanced remakes of classic Sierra On-Line adventure games and publishes them itself as free downloads.[^ref-1][^ref-18] It was founded in 2001 by Britney K. Brimhall and Christopher T. Warren as Tierra Entertainment, and adopted the AGD Interactive name on December 10, 2003.[^ref-3][^ref-2][^ref-22] Its stated aim is "to remake classic Sierra On-Line adventure games, enhance them to modern standards, and then offer them as free downloads."[^ref-18]

For the studio's full history, people and games, see [[Developers/AGD Interactive|AGD Interactive (Developer)]].

## Publishing Philosophy

AGD Interactive is an LLC.[^ref-2][^ref-19] Wikipedia describes it as a "non-profit company," and Adventure Classic Gaming called it "a nonprofit independent game development team."[^ref-3][^ref-16] Giant Bomb notes that "all their releases are completely free to download from the company's website."[^ref-10]

The remakes were made with the knowledge of Sierra's owner. In 2006 Brimhall said the team had been contacted by Vivendi Universal and that "we now have permission to create these games."[^ref-16]

## Games Published

- **2001** — [[2001 - King's Quest I VGA Remake|King's Quest I VGA Remake]] — Notes: First release, self-published freeware[^ref-3]
- **2002** — [[2002 - King's Quest II+ - Romancing the Stones|King's Quest II: Romancing the Stones]] — Notes: Expanded remake with new content, first released December 3, 2002[^ref-6][^ref-20][^ref-21]
- **2008** — [[2008 - Quest for Glory II VGA Remake|Quest for Glory II VGA Remake]] — Notes: Released "after nearly 8 years in development"; Warren began it in late 2000[^ref-17]; Engadget described it as "in development off and on for nearly seven years"[^ref-23]
- **2011** — [[2011 - King's Quest III Redux - To Heir is Human|King's Quest III Redux: To Heir is Human]] — Notes: Described by AGDI as its "most polished remake ever"[^ref-1][^ref-7]
