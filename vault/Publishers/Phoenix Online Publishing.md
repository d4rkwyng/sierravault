# Phoenix Online Publishing

<small style="color: gray">Last updated: November 11, 2026</small>

## Overview

Phoenix Online Publishing is the publishing label of Phoenix Online, the independent studio that grew out of the King's Quest fan game *The Silver Lining*.[^ref-1] Phoenix announced the label on 20 February 2014 in a press release datelined San Jose, California; MacTech described it as "the new independent publishing division of Phoenix Online Studios".[^ref-4][^ref-18][^ref-17] The company's own site says the publisher "operates in parallel with Phoenix Online Studios".[^ref-3] It publishes story-driven adventure games for PC, Mac and mobile, and MobyGames credits it on 14 games from 2013 to 2025.[^ref-4][^ref-2]

**Status (checked 11 November 2026):** the newest post on postudios.com's news blog is dated 3 September 2020 (the launch of *Bleeding Moons*), and the site's homepage was last modified on 21 March 2023 according to its HTTP header.[^ref-28] The label's Steam publisher page lists *Bleeding Moons* (3 September 2020) as its newest release there.[^ref-26][^ref-40] MobyGames credits the label with a 2025 title, *A Rite from the Stars: Remaster Edition*, but Steam lists that game's publisher as Artax Games.[^ref-30][^ref-31] This page does not know whether the label is still operating.

For the studio's full history, see [[Phoenix Online Studios|Phoenix Online Studios (Developer)]].

## Sierra Connection

Phoenix began as the volunteer team behind *The Silver Lining*, a fan-made continuation of the King's Quest series. MobyGames says the group was formed in 2004 to work on it.[^ref-1] Activision agreed on 26 June 2010 to let the game be released as long as it stayed free,[^ref-16][^ref-25] and the first episode came out on 10 July 2010.[^ref-24][^ref-16]

Phoenix also worked with Sierra designer [[Jane Jensen|Jane Jensen]]. This label published *Moebius: Empire Rising* (2014), which Phoenix Online Studios and Jensen's Pinkerton Road developed.[^ref-17][^ref-11][^ref-22] Her *Gabriel Knight: Sins of the Fathers 20th Anniversary Edition* (2014) was released by Phoenix Online Studios, with Pinkerton Road Studio as publisher on Steam, not by this label.[^ref-21][^ref-2]

## Full Catalogue (2013-2025)

MobyGames credits the label on 14 games.[^ref-2] The rest, with developers and Steam dates from each game's Steam page unless noted (none of these has a page in this vault):

- **2014**: *Lost Civilization*, Windows, 18 March 2014 per MobyGames.[^ref-29]
- **2014**: *Heroes & Legends: Conquerors of Kolhar*, developed by Cuve Games, Steam 21 August 2014.[^ref-34]
- **2015**: *Supreme League of Patriots*, developed by No Bull Intentions, Steam 29 January 2015; MobyGames lists its Issue 1 and Season Pass separately.[^ref-35][^ref-2]
- **2015**: *Morningstar: Descent to Deadrock*, developed by Red Herring Labs, Steam 17 February 2015.[^ref-36]
- **2016**: *Cornerstone: The Song of Tyrim*, developed by Overflow, Steam 26 April 2016.[^ref-33]
- **2016**: *The Last Door: Season 2 - Collector's Edition*, developed by The Game Kitchen, Steam 29 March 2016.[^ref-37]
- **2016**: *Tick's Tales*, developed by Digital Bounce House, Steam 9 June 2016.[^ref-38]
- **2018**: *A Rite from the Stars*, developed by Risin' Goat and Flying Beast Labs, Steam 19 July 2018.[^ref-39]
- **2020**: *Bleeding Moons*, developed by IAN Games, Steam 3 September 2020.[^ref-40]
- **2025**: *A Rite from the Stars: Remaster Edition*, credited to the label by MobyGames (console releases 30 January 2025, Windows 2 February 2025); Steam lists Flying Beast Labs as developer and Artax Games as publisher.[^ref-30][^ref-31]

Steam also lists the label as publisher of *Face Noir* (developed by Mad Orange, 17 October 2013) and *The Last Door: Collector's Edition* (The Game Kitchen, 20 May 2014); neither appears in MobyGames' list of 14.[^ref-41][^ref-42]

The label's 2014 launch lineup also included *The Last Door: Collector's Edition* and *Lost Civilization*, which have no Sierra connection.[^ref-4][^ref-15]

**Related (released by Phoenix Online Studios, not this label):** [[2014 - Gabriel Knight - Sins of the Fathers – 20th Anniversary Edition|Gabriel Knight: Sins of the Fathers 20th Anniversary]] (15 October 2014)[^ref-21]
