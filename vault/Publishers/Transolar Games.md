# Transolar Games

<small style="color: gray">Last updated: October 16, 2026</small>

## Overview

Transolar Games is an independent game company founded by [[Corey Cole|Corey Cole]] and [[Lori Ann Cole|Lori Ann Cole]], the designers of Sierra's *Quest for Glory* series.[^ref-1][^ref-18] Its itch.io profile says: "We develop and publish role-playing, adventure, and puzzle games for Windows, Macintosh, Linux, and mobile devices."[^ref-18] Corey Cole is the company's president.[^ref-1]

No source found gives an exact founding year. The company was already online as "TranSolar Games" in May 1999, listing Quest for Glory 1–5, Shannara and Castle of Dr. Brain among its people's games.[^ref-16] A 2008 Strange Horizons interview, titled for "Lori, Corey, and Michael Cole of Transolar Games", describes the three as "the executive team for Transolar Entertainment".[^ref-17]

## Sierra Connection

Corey and Lori Ann Cole co-designed the first four Quest for Glory games at Sierra On-Line. Lori was the sole designer of *Quest for Glory V* (1998), on which Corey worked as a programmer.[^ref-9]

Transolar's *Hero-U: Rogue to Redemption* was funded on Kickstarter between October 19 and November 20, 2012, raising $409,150 from 6,093 backers against a $400,000 goal.[^ref-2] PC Gamer summed up its road to release as "Six years, two Kickstarter campaigns, and one home equity loan later".[^ref-8] The game came out in July 2018.[^ref-1][^ref-3][^ref-5]
