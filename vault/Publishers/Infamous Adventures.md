# Infamous Adventures

<small style="color: gray">Last updated: October 26, 2026</small>

## Overview

Infamous Adventures was an amateur studio, founded in 2004[^ref-14] by Shawn Mills and Steven Alexander, that made and gave away free VGA remakes of classic Sierra On-Line adventure games. Its project teams came "from around the globe".[^ref-3] It released two remakes: *King's Quest III* in 2006 and *Space Quest II* in 2011.[^ref-2][^ref-7][^ref-6] In a site post dated 27 July 2012 the founders announced that the studio had stopped making games and that they had started a new company, [[Publishers/Infamous Quests|Infamous Quests]], to make original commercial adventure games.[^ref-3][^ref-14] The Infamous Adventures name stayed in use after that: it released version 2.0 of its Space Quest II remake on 1 January 2020,[^ref-17][^ref-23] and a King's Quest IV remake attributed to it is in development, with its only recent update a patron-only post on the Patreon of Infamous Quests, the founders' separate commercial studio.[^ref-18]

In November 2006 the studio posted a trailer for a secret project called "Project X".[^ref-22][^ref-3] On 18 June 2010 it revealed the game as *King's Quest: Kingdom of Sorrow*, based on the King's Quest novel of the same name; it was cancelled on 27 July 2012.[^ref-3]

For the studio's full history, notable games and key people, see [[Developers/Infamous Adventures|Infamous Adventures (Developer)]].

## Publishing Philosophy

Like [[Publishers/AGD Interactive|AGD Interactive]], Infamous Adventures was a non-commercial operation that released its remakes as free downloads from its own website.[^ref-3][^ref-1] Both remakes are now distributed through the studio's itch.io page.[^ref-16] Each is offered there on a name-your-own-price basis.[^ref-24][^ref-25]
