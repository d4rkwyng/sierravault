# Assemble Entertainment

<small style="color: gray">Last updated: October 16, 2026</small>

## Overview

Assemble Entertainment GmbH is a German video game publisher based in Wiesbaden.[^ref-1][^ref-2] It was founded in 2016 by Stefan Marcinek, a co-founder of Kalypso Media Group.[^ref-16][^ref-17] In September 2025 Marcinek announced that its studio Gentlymad Studios, acquired in 2017, would close at the end of that month, leaving the Wiesbaden publisher with nine staff, including managing directors Stefan Marcinek and Daniel Gubala.[^ref-17]

## Sierra Connection

Assemble Entertainment published two new Leisure Suit Larry games developed by CrazyBunch: *Wet Dreams Don't Dry* (2018) and *Wet Dreams Dry Twice* (2020).[^ref-4][^ref-5][^ref-7][^ref-9][^ref-10] The games returned the series to hand-painted 2D point-and-click play; Cubed3's review of the first said CrazyBunch "has done things properly" in that respect, but found the game "funny, but not as funny as it could, or, more importantly, as it should be."[^ref-15]

On October 8, 2026, Assemble announced on Steam that both games "will only be available on Steam until October 23. After that, both games will be removed from the store." It gave no reason, saying only: "Unfortunately, we can't share anything about his future."[^ref-18]
