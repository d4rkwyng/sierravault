# Assemble Entertainment

<small style="color: gray">Last updated: October 26, 2026</small>

## Overview

Assemble Entertainment GmbH is a German video game publisher based in Wiesbaden.[^ref-1][^ref-2] It was founded in 2016 by Stefan Marcinek, a co-founder of Kalypso Media Group.[^ref-16][^ref-17][^ref-22] Marcinek had co-founded Kalypso Media with Simon Hellwig in 2006 and sold his shares in January 2016.[^ref-22] In September 2025 Marcinek announced that its studio Gentlymad Studios, acquired in 2017, would close at the end of that month, leaving the Wiesbaden publisher with nine staff, including managing directors Stefan Marcinek and Daniel Gubala.[^ref-17]

## Sierra Connection

Assemble brought the classic Leisure Suit Larry games back to Steam: the original adventures and the *Magna Cum Laude* minigame collection went on sale there on December 22, 2017.[^ref-23] On April 30, 2025, Assemble announced that Leisure Suit Larry 1, 2, 3, 5, 6 and 7 and *Magna Cum Laude Uncut and Uncensored* would leave the Steam store, while staying in owners' libraries.[^ref-24]

Assemble Entertainment published two new Leisure Suit Larry games developed by CrazyBunch: *Wet Dreams Don't Dry* (2018) and *Wet Dreams Dry Twice* (2020).[^ref-4][^ref-5][^ref-7][^ref-9][^ref-10] *Wet Dreams Don't Dry* returned the series to hand-painted 2D point-and-click play; Cubed3's review said CrazyBunch "has done things properly" in that respect, but found the game "funny, but not as funny as it could, or, more importantly, as it should be."[^ref-15]

On October 8, 2026, Assemble announced on Steam that both games "will only be available on Steam until October 23. After that, both games will be removed from the store." It gave no reason, saying only: "Unfortunately, we can't share anything about his future."[^ref-18][^ref-25][^ref-26] Both games were delisted from Steam and GOG on October 23, 2026.[^ref-19][^ref-20] The mobile versions are no longer sold either: Delisted Games marked *Wet Dreams Dry Twice*'s Android and iOS releases as delisted,[^ref-19] and on October 26, 2026 Apple's store returned no listing for either game.[^ref-21]
