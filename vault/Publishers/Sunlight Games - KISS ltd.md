# Sunlight Games

<small style="color: gray">Last updated: October 26, 2026</small>

## Overview

Sunlight Games is a game developer and publisher based in Cologne, Germany; its own Gold Rush! news posts call it "the independent game developer Sunlight Games".[^ref-15][^ref-9][^ref-1][^ref-2] Its company page says it "was founded in 2008 and transformed into a GmbH in May 2013", with a team led by Marco Sowa-Israel.[^ref-15] The same page says that "starting in 2025, Sunlight Games will continue in a new form as a sole proprietorship", keeping its name and website, with the focus on "product support and providing information".[^ref-15] MobyGames credits it on games such as *Game Tycoon 2*, *100 Ways* and *Gangsters 1920*; its Sierra connection is the Gold Rush! franchise.[^ref-3]

KISS Ltd. is credited as publisher of *Gold Rush! Anniversary* and *Gold Rush! 2* on Metacritic and IndieDB,[^ref-17][^ref-18][^ref-19][^ref-14] while Steam lists Sunlight Games as developer and publisher of both.[^ref-1][^ref-2] MobyGames' release records for the two games (read via its API) credit Sunlight Games GmbH as publisher and do not list KISS;[^ref-20][^ref-21] its Sunlight company page counts four games "with KISS Publishing Ltd."[^ref-3] No source found describes KISS Ltd. as Sunlight's parent or owner.

## Sierra Connection

The original *[[1988 - Gold Rush|Gold Rush!]]* was designed by Ken and Doug MacNeill and released by Sierra On-Line in 1988.[^ref-6][^ref-13] About ten years later, The Software Farm, "run by the original designers, bought the rights to the game from Sierra" and republished it as *California Gold Rush*.[^ref-8] The game opens in Brooklyn in 1848, just before the California Gold Rush.[^ref-6][^ref-8]

According to Wikipedia, Sunlight Games "secured the rights and re-released the original version in July 2014".[^ref-6] Sunlight's Gold Rush! releases are the original game (*Gold Rush! Classic*), a remake (*Gold Rush! Anniversary*) and a sequel (*Gold Rush! 2*).[^ref-1][^ref-2][^ref-16]

## Games Published (Sierra Archive)

- **2014** — Gold Rush! Classic — Notes: Re-release of the 1988 original on Steam, July 25, 2014; developers credited as The Software Farm, Sunlight Games and Sierra Entertainment. Sunlight announced a boxed version for its webshop on 29 August 2014, and MobyGames lists a German release that day[^ref-7][^ref-16][^ref-9]
- **2014** — [[2014 - Gold Rush Anniversary|Gold Rush! Anniversary]] — Notes: Remake of the 1988 original; Steam release November 7, 2014[^ref-1][^ref-5]
- **2017** — [[2017 - Gold Rush 2|Gold Rush! 2]] — Notes: New sequel; Steam release April 28, 2017, German retail release June 30, 2017[^ref-2][^ref-9]
- **2019** — Gold Rush! Special Edition (Amiga) — Notes: Announced 10 October 2019 as the first release of Sunlight's retro label "RetroTainment", to be released at the Amiga34 show in Neuss, Germany; no confirmed ship date found[^ref-9]
