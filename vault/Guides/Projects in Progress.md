## Detailed Info

### Gabriel Knight 4
- **Page:** [[TBD - Gabriel Knight 4 - Five Hearts]]
- **Series:** [[1999 - Gabriel Knight 3 - Blood of the Sacred, Blood of the Damned|GK3]] → GK4
- **Team:** [[Jane Jensen]]
- **Status:** Pitch only; no Microsoft approval as of Dec 2025.
- **Latest:** (Dec 2025) [Matt Chat 565](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=UKI8t6KgAHc): Jensen described a GK4 pitch set in Salzburg, with two paths through the story ([Multiplayer.it](https://multiplayer.it/notizie/gabriel-knight-4-e-un-progetto-gia-avviato-dallautrice-jane-jensen-che-spera-di-poterlo-concludere.html): "ambientato a Salisburgo"; "due strade"). Microsoft owns the IP (via Activision) and, per Multiplayer.it's write-up of the interview, has so far shown no interest in the project despite Jensen submitting the idea several times ([Multiplayer.it](https://multiplayer.it/notizie/gabriel-knight-4-e-un-progetto-gia-avviato-dallautrice-jane-jensen-che-spera-di-poterlo-concludere.html)). Jensen also published the first chapter of a Gabriel Knight short story, "Five Hearts," in November 2024 ([Adventure Game Hotspot](https://adventuregamehotspot.com/announcement/3108/jane-jensen-releases-gabriel-knight-short-story-five-hearts)).
- **Archived fan site (2019):** [GK4Ever.com](https://web.archive.org/web/20190126092125/http://www.gk4ever.com/) (domain no longer resolves)

### Hero-U Sequel
- **Series:** [[2018 - Hero-U - Rogue to Redemption|Hero-U]] · [[2023 - Summer Daze - Tilly's Tale|Summer Daze]]
- **Team:** [[Corey Cole|Corey]] & Lori Cole ([[Transolar Games]])
- **Status:** Shelved, as of Sep 16, 2026. The Coles say they lacked the resources for the planned Hero-U sequels and changed course to the smaller Summer Daze series.
- **Latest:** (Sep 16, 2026) [Summer Daze BackerKit update](https://summerdaze.backerkit.com/hosted_preorders/project_updates): had they "unlimited time and budget to make the full originally planned Hero-U series," the Summer Daze games and Hero-U games would alternate, one prequel per hero class ("Tilly, then Shawn; Ifeyo, then Nona"), but "we don't expect to get very far in that progression." Next up is [[#Summer Daze Ifeyo's Adventure|Summer Daze: Ifeyo's Adventure]].
- **Follow:** [Patreon](https://www.patreon.com/c/coreyloricole/home)

### Quest for Infamy Roehm to Ruin
- **Page:** [[TBD - Quest for Infamy - Roehm to Ruin]]
- **Series:** Prequel to [[2014 - Quest for Infamy|Quest for Infamy]]
- **Team:** [[Infamous Quests]]
- **Status:** Voice recording for Mr. Roehm booked for May 2026; no public confirmation since that it took place, as of Oct 26, 2026. The developer reports waning momentum.

On Sep 28, 2026 he posted that Infamous Quests would have a booth at RetroGameCon in Syracuse that weekend ([Bluesky](https://bsky.app/profile/infamousquests.bsky.social/post/3mwlj77fgcs2r)); itch.io still lists Roehm to Ruin as "In development".

### AGD Interactive Updates

- **Status:** KQ1 v4.2 is still at Release Candidate 1; the forum thread was last active in August 2026. KQ2+ has had no new version since v3.1/3.1b (2010–2011).
