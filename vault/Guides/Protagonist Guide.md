# Protagonist Guide

<small style="color: gray">Last updated: October 26, 2026</small>

Sierra's adventure games are defined by their memorable protagonists. This guide covers the heroes and player roles of Sierra's series, including adventure, simulation and strategy games, later sequels by other studios and Sierra alumni, and fan games. Protagonist details are sourced on each linked game page; the footnotes below cover the points this guide states directly.

---

## Legendary Heroes

### Roger Wilco (Space Quest)
The galaxy's most unlikely hero—a janitor who repeatedly saves the universe through luck, persistence, and an uncanny ability to survive his own incompetence. In November 1996 *Computer Gaming World* ranked him fifth among its 15 Most Memorable Game Heroes, "a schlemiel who accidentally accomplished heroic ends".[^ref-23]

- **1986** — [[1986 - Space Quest - The Sarien Encounter|Space Quest: The Sarien Encounter]]
- **1987** — [[1987 - Space Quest II - Vohaul's Revenge|Space Quest II: Vohaul's Revenge]]
- **1989** — [[1989 - Space Quest III - The Pirates of Pestulon|Space Quest III: The Pirates of Pestulon]]
- **1991** — [[1991 - Space Quest I - Roger Wilco in the Sarien Encounter|Space Quest I VGA]]
- **1991** — [[1991 - Space Quest IV - Roger Wilco and the Time Rippers|Space Quest IV: Roger Wilco and the Time Rippers]]
- **1993** — [[1993 - Space Quest V - The Next Mutation|Space Quest V: The Next Mutation]]
- **1995** — [[1995 - Space Quest 6 - Roger Wilco in the Spinal Frontier|Space Quest 6: Roger Wilco in the Spinal Frontier]]

**Fan Games:** [[2001 - Space Quest - The Lost Chapter|The Lost Chapter]], [[2003 - Space Quest 0 - Replicated|SQ0: Replicated]], [[2011 - Space Quest II VGA Remake|SQ2 VGA]], [[2011 - Space Quest - Vohaul Strikes Back|Vohaul Strikes Back]], [[2012 - Space Quest - Incinerations|Incinerations]]

### The Daventry Royal Family (King's Quest)

#### Sir Graham / King Graham
From brave knight to wise king. Graham is the hero of King's Quest I, II and V and the 2015 reimagining, and appears throughout the saga.

- **1984** — [[1984 - King's Quest - Quest for the Crown|King's Quest I: Quest for the Crown]]
- **1985** — [[1985 - King's Quest II - Romancing the Throne|King's Quest II: Romancing the Throne]]
- **1990** — [[1990 - Roberta Williams' King's Quest I - Quest for the Crown|King's Quest I SCI remake]] (SCI01, 16-colour EGA)[^ref-22]
- **1990** — [[1990 - King's Quest V - Absence Makes the Heart Go Yonder|King's Quest V]]
- **2015** — [[2015 - King's Quest|King's Quest (2015)]]

**Fan Games:** [[2001 - King's Quest I VGA Remake|KQ1 VGA Remake]], [[2002 - King's Quest II+ - Romancing the Stones|KQ2+]], [[2010 - The Silver Lining|The Silver Lining]]

#### Gwydion / Prince Alexander
Kidnapped as an infant, he escapes slavery to reclaim his birthright and later rescue his true love.

- **1986** — [[1986 - King's Quest III - To Heir Is Human|King's Quest III: To Heir Is Human]]
- **1992** — [[1992 - King's Quest VI - Heir Today, Gone Tomorrow|King's Quest VI: Heir Today, Gone Tomorrow]]

**Fan Games:** [[2006 - King's Quest III Remake (Infamous Adventures)|KQ3 VGA (Infamous)]], [[2011 - King's Quest III Redux - To Heir is Human|KQ3 Redux]]

#### Princess Rosella
The adventurous princess who saved her father's life and later escaped a troll king, King Otar, who wanted to marry her.[^ref-13]

- **1988** — [[1988 - King's Quest IV - The Perils of Rosella|King's Quest IV: The Perils of Rosella]]
- **1994** — [[1994 - King's Quest VII - The Princeless Bride|King's Quest VII]] (with Queen Valanice)

**Fan Games:** [[2021 - King's Quest IV Retold|KQ4 Retold]], [[TBD - King's Quest IV Remake (Infamous Adventures)|KQ4 Remake (Infamous Adventures, in development)]]

#### Connor
Not a royal but a simple peasant and fighter, who picks up a fragment of the shattered Mask of Eternity and must find the other pieces to face Lucreto.[^ref-26]

- **1998** — [[1998 - King's Quest - Mask of Eternity|King's Quest: Mask of Eternity]]

### The Hero (Quest for Glory)
A customizable champion who starts as a Fighter, Magic User or Thief; from Quest for Glory II on, an honorable hero can earn the title of Paladin—your choices shape their legend.[^ref-5]

- **1989** — [[1989 - Quest for Glory I - So You Want to Be a Hero|Quest for Glory I]] — Classes: Fighter / Magic User / Thief
- **1990** — [[1990 - Quest for Glory II - Trial by Fire|Quest for Glory II: Trial by Fire]] — Classes: Fighter / Magic User / Thief (honorable heroes are awarded the Paladin title at the end)[^ref-5]
- **1992** — [[1992 - Quest for Glory I - So You Want to Be a Hero VGA|Quest for Glory I VGA]] — Classes: Fighter / Magic User / Thief
- **1992** — [[1992 - Quest for Glory III - Wages of War|Quest for Glory III: Wages of War]] — Classes: Fighter / Magic User / Thief / Paladin
- **1993** — [[1993 - Quest for Glory IV - Shadows of Darkness|Quest for Glory IV: Shadows of Darkness]] — Classes: Fighter / Magic User / Thief / Paladin
- **1998** — [[1998 - Quest for Glory V - Dragon Fire|Quest for Glory V: Dragon Fire]] — Classes: Fighter / Wizard / Thief / Paladin

**Fan Games:** [[2008 - Quest for Glory II VGA Remake|QFG2 VGA Remake]]

New Orleans bookstore owner turned supernatural investigator, unraveling dark family secrets and ancient mysteries. *Computer Gaming World* ranked him second among its 15 Most Memorable Game Heroes in November 1996.[^ref-23]

- **1993** — [[1993 - Gabriel Knight - Sins of the Fathers|Gabriel Knight: Sins of the Fathers]] — Also Starring: Grace Nakimura (non-playable assistant)
- **1995** — [[1995 - The Beast Within - A Gabriel Knight Mystery|The Beast Within]] — Also Starring: Grace Nakimura
- **1999** — [[1999 - Gabriel Knight 3 - Blood of the Sacred, Blood of the Damned|Gabriel Knight 3]] — Also Starring: Grace Nakimura
- **2014** — [[2014 - Gabriel Knight - Sins of the Fathers – 20th Anniversary Edition|GK1 20th Anniversary]] — Also Starring: Grace Nakimura (non-playable assistant)

### Laura Bow
Tulane University student and aspiring journalist turned New York newspaper reporter, solving murders with wit and determination.[^ref-14][^ref-15] *Computer Gaming World* ranked her 13th among its 15 Most Memorable Game Heroes in November 1996, as "Roberta Williams's journalism-student, cub-reporter protagonist".[^ref-23]

- **1989** — [[1989 - The Colonel's Bequest|The Colonel's Bequest]]
- **1992** — [[1992 - The Dagger of Amon Ra|The Dagger of Amon Ra]]

### Gobliiins Series

#### Oups, Asgard, and Ignatius
Three goblins with unique abilities who must work together to solve puzzles.

- **1991** — [[1991 - Gobliiins|Gobliiins]]
- **2023** — [[2023 - Gobliiins 5 - The Morgloton Invasion|Gobliiins 5: The Morgloton Invasion]]

#### Fingus and Winkle
A comedic duo on a mission to save their prince—returning after 30+ years for a new adventure.

- **1992** — [[1992 - Gobliins 2 - The Prince Buffoon|Gobliins 2: The Prince Buffoon]]
- **2026** — [[2026 - Gobliins 6|Gobliins 6]] (released February 2026)[^ref-2]

### Dr. Brain Series
Various protagonists challenge the eccentric Dr. Brain's puzzles across multiple games.

- **1991** — [[1991 - Castle of Dr. Brain|Castle of Dr. Brain]] — Protagonist: Job applicant
- **1992** — [[1992 - The Island of Dr. Brain|The Island of Dr. Brain]] — Protagonist: Dr. Brain's assistant
- **1995** — [[1995 - The Lost Mind of Dr. Brain|The Lost Mind of Dr. Brain]] — Protagonist: Player, guided by Dr. Brain's niece Elaina (an experiment mixed up the minds of Dr. Brain and his lab rat Rathbone)[^ref-16]
- **1996** — [[1996 - The Time Warp of Dr. Brain|The Time Warp of Dr. Brain]] — Protagonist: Player, rescuing Dr. Brain from the space-time discontinuum[^ref-24]
- **1999** — [[1999 - Dr. Brain - Action Reaction|Dr. Brain: Action Reaction]] — Protagonist: Player, captured by S.P.O.R.E. along with Dr. Brain (Wikipedia; MobyGames instead says the player takes the role of Dr. Brain)[^ref-17][^ref-18]

### Gold Rush! — Jerrod Wilson
A Brooklyn newspaperman who heads west during the California Gold Rush to find his long-lost brother Jake.[^ref-11]

- **1988** — [[1988 - Gold Rush|Gold Rush!]]
- **2014** — [[2014 - Gold Rush Anniversary|Gold Rush! Anniversary]]
- **2017** — [[2017 - Gold Rush 2|Gold Rush! 2]] (story follows both Jerrod and Jake[^ref-12]; the developer's FAQ names Jerrod as the protagonist[^ref-21])

### Homeworld Series — The S'jet Family
Kushan scientists who lead their people's fleets and expeditions. Cataclysm is the exception: it follows the fleet of Kiith Somtaaw rather than a S'jet.

- **1999** — [[1999 - Homeworld|Homeworld]] — Protagonist: Karan S'jet
- **2000** — [[2000 - Homeworld - Cataclysm|Homeworld: Cataclysm]] — Protagonist: Kiith Somtaaw fleet commander, aboard the mining vessel Kuun-Lan[^ref-9]
- **2003** — [[2003 - Homeworld 2|Homeworld 2]] — Protagonist: Karan S'jet
- **2016** — [[2016 - Homeworld - Deserts of Kharak|Homeworld: Deserts of Kharak]] — Protagonist: Rachel S'jet
- **2024** — [[2024 - Homeworld 3|Homeworld 3]] — Protagonist: Imogen S'jet

### Inca — El Dorado
A mystical Incan warrior fighting to reclaim his people's legacy. Inca II opens with the player as his son Atahualpa before returning to El Dorado.[^ref-19]

- **1992** — [[1992 - Inca|Inca]]
- **1993** — [[1993 - Inca II - Wiracocha|Inca II: Wiracocha]]

### Conquests — King Arthur & Robin Hood
Legendary heroes in Christy Marx's historical adventures.

- **1990** — [[1990 - Conquests of Camelot - The Search for the Grail|Conquests of Camelot]] — Protagonist: King Arthur
- **1991** — [[1991 - Conquests of the Longbow - The Legend of Robin Hood|Conquests of the Longbow]] — Protagonist: Robin Hood

---

### Krondor — Multiple Heroes
Warriors and mages of Raymond E. Feist's Riftwar world (the Krondor games) and of Ramar, the original world Sierra created for Betrayal in Antara after losing the Riftwar rights.[^ref-3]

- **1993** — [[1993 - Betrayal at Krondor|Betrayal at Krondor]] — Protagonists: fighters Locklear, James and Gorath; magicians Pug, Owyn and Patrus[^ref-6]
- **1997** — [[1997 - Betrayal in Antara|Betrayal in Antara]] — Protagonists: William Escobar, Aren Cordelaine, Kaelyn Usher[^ref-3]
- **1998** — [[1998 - Return to Krondor|Return to Krondor]] — Protagonist: Squire James (party members Jazhara, William, Kendaric, Solon)[^ref-7]

- **2001** — [[2001 - Tribes 2|Tribes 2]] — Protagonist: Tribal Warriors

## City-Building and Strategy Protagonists
Sierra's city-building games and the Lords of the Realm strategy series feature unnamed rulers managing civilizations. Caesar (1992) and Lords of the Realm (1994) were made by Impressions Games before David Lester sold the studio to Sierra On-Line in 1995.[^ref-27]

- [[1992 - Caesar|Caesar]], [[1995 - Caesar II|Caesar II]], [[1998 - Caesar III|Caesar III]], [[2006 - Caesar IV|Caesar IV]] — Series: **Caesar**
- [[1999 - Pharaoh|Pharaoh]] — Series: **Pharaoh**
- [[2000 - Zeus - Master of Olympus|Zeus: Master of Olympus]] — Series: **Zeus**
- [[2002 - Emperor - Rise of the Middle Kingdom|Emperor: Rise of the Middle Kingdom]] — Series: **Emperor**
- [[1994 - Lords of the Realm|Lords of the Realm]], [[1996 - Lords of the Realm II|Lords of the Realm II]], [[2004 - Lords of the Realm III|Lords of the Realm III]] — Series: **Lords of the Realm**
