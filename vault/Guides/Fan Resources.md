# Fan & Community Resources

<small style="color: gray">Last updated: October 19, 2026</small>

Want to dig deeper than these Markdown notes? These sites preserve interviews, downloads, and discussions across the Sierra fandom.

## General Sierra Hubs
- [SierraHelp.com](https://sierrahelp.com/) - Archive of official patches, installers, and troubleshooting guides for classic Sierra games.
- [SierraHelp Forums](https://forums.sierrahelp.com/) - Discussion board for Sierra tech support, fan games, and preservation.
- [Sierra Chest](https://www.sierrachest.com/) - Database of Sierra games, companies and people, with Sierra magazines, catalogues, manuals, and hint books. ([Patreon](https://www.patreon.com/sierrachest))
- [Sierra Gamers](https://www.sierragamers.com/) - Ken Williams' official site preserving Sierra history with game manuals and [forums](https://www.sierragamers.com/forums/).
- [QuestStudios Archive](http://www.midimusicadventures.com/queststudios/) - Quest Studios' Sierra soundtrack recordings (King's Quest, Space Quest, Leisure Suit Larry, Quest for Glory and more), now hosted by the MIDI Music Adventure Show.
- [ScummVM Wiki - Sierra](https://wiki.scummvm.org/index.php?title=Sierra) - Technical documentation for AGI/SCI engine compatibility.

## Series-Specific Sites

### King's Quest
- [King's Quest Omnipedia (Fandom)](https://kingsquest.fandom.com/wiki/King's_Quest_Omnipedia) - Comprehensive wiki covering all KQ games, characters, and lore.
- [Daventry Chronicles](https://frodobaggins.wixsite.com/daventrychronicles) - Fan site dedicated to King's Quest history and community.

### Space Quest
- [SpaceQuest.Net](https://spacequest.net/) - Archive for Two Guys content, concept art, and fan game downloads.
- [Space Quest Omnipedia (Fandom)](https://spacequest.fandom.com/wiki/Space_Quest_Omnipedia) - Detailed wiki with timelines, character bios, and fan content.

### Quest for Glory
- [Quest for More Glory (Archive)](https://web.archive.org/web/20240322150642/http://www.questformoreglory.com/) - Strategy guides, hero import tools, and Hero-U updates.

### Leisure Suit Larry
- [Larry Laffer Dot Net](http://larrylaffer.net/) - LSL fan site with walkthroughs, music pages, manuals and hint books, and LSL2's in-game Black Book. (Its forum link points to the SierraHelp forums.)
- [Leisure Suit Larry Omnipedia (archived 2024)](https://web.archive.org/web/20240907115234/https://leisuresuitlarry.fandom.com/wiki/Leisure_Suit_Larry_Omnipedia) - Wiki covering LSL games and characters; the Fandom wiki has since been taken down.
- [Al Lowe's Humor Site](http://allowe.com/) - Series creator's official site with [Larry Links](http://allowe.com/games/larry/even-more-larry/larry-links.html), game history, and CyberJoke 3000 newsletter.

### Gabriel Knight
- [Gabriel Knight Omnipedia](https://gabrielknight.fandom.com/wiki/Gabriel_Knight_Omnipedia) - Fandom wiki covering the games, demos, books and characters, including a 20th Anniversary timeline and a canon policy that keeps the original, novel and remake storylines apart.

## Forums & Communities
- [Reddit r/Sierra](https://www.reddit.com/r/Sierra/) - General Sierra discussion, tech support, and nostalgia.
- [Reddit r/leisuresuitlarry](https://www.reddit.com/r/leisuresuitlarry/) - Fan community for classic LSL discussion.
- [AGDI](https://www.agdinteractive.com/) & [Infamous Adventures](https://infamousadventures.itch.io/) - Fan hubs hosting remakes, voice packs, and developer diaries. (Note: infamous-adventures.com now redirects to their Itch.io page.)

## Interviews & Podcasts
- [Space Quest Historian Podcast](https://spacequesthistorian.com/podcast/) - Troels Pleimert's podcast, three seasons (2013–2016); the third season focused on interviews with game developers. ([Apple Podcasts listing, archived 2025](https://web.archive.org/web/20250815224458/https://podcasts.apple.com/us/podcast/space-quest-historian-podcast-by-troels-pleimert-tech/id739423439))
- [Adventure Game Hotspot](https://adventuregamehotspot.com/) - Modern coverage of Sierra-related Kickstarters and retrospectives. ([Podcast episode: "Our Favorite Sierra Adventure Game Worlds", Aug 2024](https://podcasts.apple.com/us/podcast/our-favorite-sierra-adventure-game-worlds/id1638963659?i=1000665661924))

## Developer Studios (Post-Sierra)
- [Cygnus Entertainment](https://cygnusentertainment.com/) - Ken & Roberta Williams' studio behind *Colossal Cave 3D Adventure*. ([History](https://cygnusentertainment.com/history/))
- [Guys from Andromeda](https://guysfromandromeda.com/) - Scott Murphy & Mark Crowe's studio; SpaceVenture was released on [Steam](https://store.steampowered.com/app/1374960/) in December 2025.

## Preservation Projects
- [Archive.org InterAction Magazine Collection](https://archive.org/details/interactionmagazine) - Scans of Sierra's InterAction magazine.
- [ScummVM](https://www.scummvm.org/) & [DOSBox](https://www.dosbox.com/) communities - Compatibility notes (and ScummVM daily builds) for running AGI/SCI titles on modern systems.
- [ScummVM Wiki - AGI](https://wiki.scummvm.org/index.php/AGI) - Technical documentation for Sierra's Adventure Game Interpreter.
