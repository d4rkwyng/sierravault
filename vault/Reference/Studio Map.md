# Studio Map

<small style="color: gray">Last updated: October 26, 2026</small>

## Overview

This is an index of the game-development studios represented in the Sierra extended catalogue — Sierra's own internal studios and labels, the studios Sierra acquired, partner studios whose games Sierra published, studios founded by Sierra alumni, and the fan and independent studios that have produced remakes and spiritual successors. Each row gives founding and closure dates, status and notable credits, with the source for each row. A dash (—) means no checked source gives the value.

Used together with [[Corporate Lineage]] and the per-studio Developer pages, this gives a single-page answer to questions like "Which studios contributed to the Sierra catalogue, and when were they each active?"
