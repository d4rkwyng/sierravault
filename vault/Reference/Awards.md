# Awards

<small style="color: gray">Last updated: October 16, 2026</small>

## Overview

Sierra On-Line games and creators collected industry awards from the late 1980s onward — from *Computer Gaming World*'s annual awards to King's Quest's 2020 induction into the World Video Game Hall of Fame. This page indexes the awards that a period or primary source confirms, organized by award body and then by year. It is the cross-reference for any vault page that mentions an award won.

This is not a comprehensive list of every nomination — it focuses on category wins, notable nominations, lifetime honours and hall-of-fame inductions that a cited source documents. Every row below was checked against the source in its footnote (October 2026); earlier rows that no source supported were removed.

## World Video Game Hall of Fame (The Strong Museum of Play)

The Strong's World Video Game Hall of Fame judges eligible games on four criteria: icon status, longevity, geographical reach, and influence.[^ref-strong-criteria]

## Computer Gaming World Awards

*Computer Gaming World* (1981–2006) ran annual Game of the Year awards (renamed the Premier Awards in 1994) and kept a reader-driven Hall of Fame. Sierra won the adventure category in the 1990 and 1991 awards and shared it in 1994, and took the overall Game of the Year in 1996; it also won repeatedly in sports, simulation and role-playing through its Dynamix studio.

### CGW Game of the Year / Premier Awards

| June 1994 (#119) | [[1993 - Gabriel Knight - Sins of the Fathers\|Gabriel Knight: Sins of the Fathers]] | Premier Adventure Game of the Year, co-winner with Day of the Tentacle[^ref-cgw-119] |

## Retrospective "best of" lists

### Adventure Gamers Top 100 (2011)

Adventure Gamers' 2011 list of the 100 best adventure games, as reproduced by Kotaku, ranked *Gabriel Knight: The Beast Within* #3, *King's Quest VI* #13 and *Gabriel Knight: Sins of the Fathers* #16.[^ref-kotaku-ag100]

### CGW retrospective rankings

CGW's "150 Best Games of All Time" (issue 148, November 1996) included Sierra-family titles such as Red Baron, Gabriel Knight 2, The Incredible Machine, Leisure Suit Larry, Aces of the Deep, Quest for Glory and King's Quest V.[^ref-cgw-best]
